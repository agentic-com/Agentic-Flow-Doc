## What makes workflows "intelligent"?

Traditional automation follows fixed rules: "If this happens, do that." Intelligent workflows can reason, adapt, and make decisions based on context and goals.

**Traditional Workflow:**
1.  Go to web page
2.  Extract specific text
3.  Save to file

**Intelligent Workflow:**
1.  **Analyze** the web page content
2.  **Decide** what information is actually important
3.  **Extract** only the relevant insights
4.  **Categorize** and store the results intelligently

## Key capabilities you'll master

**Smart Document Processing**: AI that understands content meaning, not just text patterns - perfect for analyzing articles, extracting insights, and processing any written content intelligently.

**Adaptive Decision Making**: Workflows that adjust behavior based on results and context - your automation gets smarter over time and handles unexpected situations gracefully.

**Intelligent Data Extraction**: AI that knows what information is valuable and relevant - no more rigid selectors that break when websites change.

**Contextual Memory**: Systems that remember and learn from previous interactions - build AI assistants that get better at helping you over time.

**Goal-Oriented Automation**: AI agents that work toward objectives, not just execute steps - describe what you want accomplished and let AI figure out how to do it.

**Multi-Agent Coordination**: Orchestrate multiple AI specialists working together - research agents, analysis agents, and writing agents collaborating on complex projects.

## Real-world applications

**Intelligent Research Assistant**: AI that can plan research strategies, gather information from multiple sources, analyze findings, and create comprehensive reports.

**Adaptive Content Monitor**: Systems that understand what content changes actually matter, filtering out noise and highlighting important updates.

**Smart Customer Support**: AI that searches your knowledge base, understands customer intent, and provides accurate answers with source citations.

**Dynamic Competitive Intelligence**: Agents that monitor competitors, adapt to website changes, and provide ongoing strategic insights.
