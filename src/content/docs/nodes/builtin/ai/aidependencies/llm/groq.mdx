## What it does

Chat Groq is a model you plug into the **Model** slot of an AI node, such as the [Tools Agent](/nodes/builtin/ai/aiagents/toolsagentnode/) or [Basic LLM Chain](/nodes/builtin/ai/aiagents/basicllmchainnode/). It does not run on its own and has no output. When the AI node runs, it sends its messages to Groq with your key and gets the reply back.

- **Temperature** is 0 when you leave it empty, so answers are focused and repeatable.
- Replies are capped at **Max Tokens** (4096 by default).
- A failed request is retried up to **Max Retries** times (2 by default). A request that gets no answer within 60 seconds fails.

## When to use it

- Speed matters: a quick reply while you wait, or many items in a loop.
- You want open models such as Llama without running them yourself.
- Classifying or tagging lots of short texts cheaply.

## Example workflow

1. A [Context Menu](/nodes/builtin/trigger/contextmenu/) trigger on selected text.
2. [Text Classifier](/nodes/builtin/ai/aiagents/textclassifiernode/) with **Text** `{{ $input.selectionText }}` and **Categories** `bug, feature request, question`.
3. Chat Groq in its **Model** slot, **Model** `llama-3.3-70b-versatile`.
4. [Browser Notification](/nodes/extension/browsernotification/) with **Message** `{{ $input.category }}`.

## Common issues

- **"Model parameter is required and cannot be empty".** Fill in **Model**.
- **A "model decommissioned" or "model not found" error.** Groq retired the model. Pick a current one from its model list.
- **A 429 "rate limit" error.** You sent too many requests or tokens per minute for your plan. Slow the workflow down (fewer items at once) or upgrade the plan.
- **A 401 error.** The key is wrong or revoked. Create a new key and update the credential.
