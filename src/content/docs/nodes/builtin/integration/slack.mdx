Integrate with Slack to perform operations on channels, messages, users, and files.

## What it does

Sends messages, manages channels, and performs operations within your Slack workspace.

## When to use it

Use this node to automate Slack notifications, post messages, create channels, or retrieve Slack data in your workflows.
