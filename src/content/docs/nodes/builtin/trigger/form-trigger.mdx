Build a form in the workflow. When it is filled in and sent, the answers start a run.

## What it does

This trigger is page 1 of a form. Press **Run** on the workflow and the form opens in a sheet of the side panel; **Open in a window** shows it as a centred card in its own window. Sending it starts a run, and the answers become this trigger's output, one field per answer.

Need more than one page?

- Add **Pages** to the trigger: each page has its own fields, with **Back** and **Next**. Everything is sent once, at the end.
- Or put [In-Page Form](/nodes/builtin/flow/in-page-form/) steps later in the workflow. They show in the same window, one after the other, while the run continues. The person sees "Earlier answers are already sent" when they can't go back.
- End with a [Form ending](/nodes/builtin/flow/form-ending/) step to show a thank-you message, open a page, or close the form. Without one, the form says **Done** (or **Something went wrong** when the run fails).

Answers are checked before the run starts: required fields, emails, links, numbers within their min and max. A wrong answer is marked at its field and nothing is sent.

:::note[This browser only]
The form opens in your AWFlow, in this browser. It is not a public link others can open.
:::

## When to use it

- A request form you fill in yourself or at a colleague's desk: a bug report, an expense, a content brief.
- A guided checklist: page 1 asks the basics, later steps ask follow-ups based on the answers.
- Collecting the inputs of a workflow in a friendly way instead of editing step settings each time.

Each field has a **name** (the key in the output), a **label**, a **type** and options such as **required**, **placeholder**, **help**, a **default** and **min/max**. Types: **Short text**, **Long text**, **Number**, **Email**, **Link**, **Date**, **Yes / no**, **Dropdown**, **Choice**, **Checkboxes** (several options) and **Hidden** (a value from an expression, sent with the answers). Dropdown, Choice and Checkboxes take a list of **Choices**.

**Give up after** ends a form run that nobody finishes. It shows once the form has 2 or more pages (a one-page form never waits): pick **Never** (it waits until you cancel it), **1 hour**, **1 day**, **1 week** or **Custom…** (in hours, at most 30 days). New form triggers start at 1 day. The deadline counts from the start of the run and covers every page; when it passes, the run ends as **Canceled** with *Form not completed · gave up after 1 day*.

You can also open a form without the editor: in the right-click menu, form workflows end with **…** and open their form instead of running at once, and the [notch](/app/assistant-notch/#whats-in-the-panel) has a **Forms** block. The form opens in the side panel when it is open, else in a small window.

When you press **Run step** in the editor, the form's default values stand in for answers, so you can build the rest of the workflow without filling it in each time.

## Example workflow

1. **When a form is submitted** with fields *email* (email, required), *topic* (choice) and *details* (long text).
2. [Switch](/nodes/builtin/flow/switch/) routes by `{{ $input.topic }}`.
3. [Slack](/nodes/builtin/integration/slack/) posts the request to the right channel.
4. [Form ending](/nodes/builtin/flow/form-ending/): "Thanks, it's filed".

## Common issues

- **"Add at least one field to the form."**: the trigger has no fields yet.
- **The form doesn't open**: open the workflow and press **Run**. The form opens from the workflow, not from a link.
- **Nothing happens after Send**: check **Runs** for a failed run; the form shows **Something went wrong** when the run fails.
