Run the workflow automatically at set times: every few minutes, daily at 9:00, every Monday, on the 1st of the month.

## What it does

Schedule starts the workflow on the rules you set, while the workflow is **On** and your browser is open. Each rule picks **Run every** and then asks only for what that choice needs:

| Run every | You set |
| --- | --- |
| **Seconds** | **Every how many seconds** (browsers run alarms at most every 30 seconds; below 30, an amber note says so) |
| **Minutes** | **Every how many minutes** |
| **Hours** | **Every how many hours** and, optionally, **Trigger at Minute** |
| **Days** | **Every how many days**, **At (hour)**, **Trigger at Minute** |
| **Weeks** | **Every how many weeks**, the weekdays, **At (hour)**, **Trigger at Minute** |
| **Months** | **Every how many months**, **Trigger at Day of Month**, **At (hour)**, **Trigger at Minute** |
| **Custom (cron)** | a **Cron rule** |

A new Schedule step starts ready: every day at 09:00. Hours read *12 AM (midnight)* and *12 PM (noon)*; a rule with an hour or minute out of range isn't scheduled and shows in **Ready to run**.

Add several rules to combine schedules, for example weekdays at 9:00 plus Saturdays at noon.

### Timezone

Hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and cron rules take an optional **Timezone** (for example *Europe/Paris*). Empty means your browser's time. Set one zone for the whole workflow in [Workflow settings](/app/workflows/settings/#running); a rule's own zone wins. Schedules stay on the same clock time when daylight saving time changes: a time skipped by the change runs at the first minute after it, a repeated hour runs once.

### Custom (cron)

**Custom (cron)** takes a 5-part cron rule: minute, hour, day of month, month, weekday. Lists (`1,15`), ranges (`1-5`), steps (`*/15`), names (`MON`, `JAN`), `L` (last day) and shortcuts like `@daily` work. The field shows the rule in plain words, the next 3 runs (and your own time when the zone differs), and ready-made presets. A 6-part rule with seconds is refused: schedules run at most once a minute. Runs missed while the browser was closed aren't replayed.

```txt title="Cron rule"
0 9 * * 1-5     every weekday at 09:00
*/15 8-18 * * * every 15 minutes from 08:00 to 18:59
0 8 L * *       at 08:00 on the last day of each month
```

## When to use it

- Daily reports, digests and reminders.
- Regular checks: a price, a page, an inbox.
- Routines that run an agent at set times, with [Run agent](/nodes/builtin/ai/aiagents/runagentnode/).

## Example workflow

1. Schedule with one rule: **Days**, **Every how many days** 1, **At (hour)** 8 AM, **Trigger at Minute** 0.
2. [RSS Feed Read](/nodes/builtin/core/rss-feed-read/) fetches a feed.
3. [Limit](/nodes/builtin/datatransformation/limit/) keeps the 5 newest posts.
4. [Telegram](/nodes/builtin/integration/telegram/) sends them to you.

## Common issues

- **The workflow never runs on its own.** Turn the workflow **On**: schedules only run for workflows that are On, while the browser is open. To test, press **Run** on the canvas.
- **A seconds schedule runs every 30 seconds.** Browsers run alarms at most every 30 seconds, so a shorter interval runs every 30 s.
- **The cron rule is refused.** Check it has 5 parts; the message under the field says what's wrong in plain words.
- **A monthly schedule skipped a month.** That month has no such day (for example the 31st). Pick a day that every month has, such as the 1st or the 28th.
