Move a date back by a number of years, months, days, hours or other units.

## What it does

Subtract From A Date takes **Date to subtract from**, removes **Duration** of the chosen **Time unit to subtract**, and writes the new date into a field named by **Output field name**.

- Units: year, quarter (3 months), month, week (7 days), day, hour, minute, second.
- The calculation uses your computer's local time zone.
- Going back a month from the 31st rolls over when the earlier month is shorter: 31 March minus 1 month gives 3 March (or 2 March in a leap year), not 28 February.

## When to use it

- Build the start of a "last 7 days" or "last 24 hours" window.
- Work out when a reminder should fire before a deadline.
- Find a date one year ago for a comparison.

## Example workflow

Keep only feed items from the last 24 hours:

1. [RSS Feed Read](/nodes/builtin/core/rss-feed-read/), **URL** of a news feed.
2. [Split Out](/nodes/builtin/datatransformation/splitout/), **Field To Split Out** `items`, **Output field name** `article`, so each article becomes its own item.
3. [Get Current Date](/nodes/builtin/datatransformation/datetime/getcurrentdate/), **Output field name** `now`, **Include input fields** on.
4. Subtract From A Date, **Date to subtract from** `{{ $input.now }}`, **Time unit to subtract** `day`, **Duration** `1`, **Output field name** `since`, **Include input fields** on.
5. [Filter](/nodes/builtin/flow/filter/): `{{ $input.article.isoDate }}` is after `{{ $input.since }}`, type **Date-Time**.

## Common issues

- **The output is "Invalid Date" or empty.** The incoming value is not a date the browser understands. Use an ISO date such as `2026-03-01`.
- **The month result lands in the wrong month.** Subtracting months from the 29th to the 31st can roll forward into the next month. Round the date down to `month` first with [Round a Date](/nodes/builtin/datatransformation/datetime/roundadate/) when you only care about the month.
- **The new date is missing after the node.** With **Include input fields** on, an incoming field with the same name as **Output field name** replaces the result. Use a different name.
