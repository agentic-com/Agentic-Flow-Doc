Pause the workflow for a set time, then carry on with the same data.

## What it does

Wait holds the run for **Amount** × **Unit** (the unit is **Seconds** unless you change it), then passes the data it received straight through, unchanged.

It pauses **once for everything that arrives**, not once per item. Ten items reaching a 5-second Wait leave together after 5 seconds. To pause between items, put Wait inside a [Loop](/nodes/builtin/flow/loop/) or after [Split in Batches](/nodes/builtin/flow/splitinbatches/).

How it waits depends on the length:

- **Under 1 minute**: a simple timer, precise to the millisecond. Closing the tab cancels it.
- **1 minute or more, in the browser extension**: a durable wait. The run is saved and resumes at the deadline, even if you close the tab or restart the browser. It resumes within about a minute of the deadline and can last up to **30 days**. Meanwhile the run shows as **waiting** in the Executions pane.
- **In the web app**, every wait uses the simple timer, so a long wait does not survive a closed tab.

## When to use it

- Space out requests so a service does not rate-limit you.
- Give a page or an external job time to finish before the next step.
- Send a follow-up later: "wait 2 days, then remind me".

To wait until something is actually ready rather than for a fixed time, use [Poll Until](/nodes/builtin/flow/polluntil/) or [Wait For Element](/nodes/extension/ui/wait-for-element/).

## Inputs and settings

{/* AUTO:settings:start */}
| Setting | Type | Default | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Amount** | Number |  | Yes | How long to wait. Waits of a minute or more are durable — the run survives a closed tab and resumes at the deadline (up to 30 days). Shorter waits run in-memory. |
| **Unit** | Choice | Seconds | Yes | The unit of time to wait. Options: Milliseconds, Seconds, Minutes, Hours. |
{/* AUTO:settings:end */}

Waits longer than 30 days are cut to 30 days.

## Outputs

{/* AUTO:outputs:start */}
One output port.
{/* AUTO:outputs:end */}

The data that came in goes out unchanged.

## Example workflow

1. A [Context Menu](/nodes/builtin/trigger/contextmenu/) trigger on a link.
2. Wait **2** **Hours**.
3. A [Browser Notification](/nodes/extension/browsernotification/) reminds you to read the link.

## Troubleshooting

- **A long wait never finished.** It ran in the web app, or was shorter than a minute, and the tab was closed. Run long waits from the extension.
- **The run resumed a little late.** Durable waits resume within about a minute of the deadline. For precise short delays, keep the wait under a minute.
- **All items arrived at once after the wait.** That is expected: Wait pauses once for the whole input. Use a Loop for per-item pauses.
