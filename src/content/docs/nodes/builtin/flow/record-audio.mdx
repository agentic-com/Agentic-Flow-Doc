Pause the workflow, record audio from the microphone, and continue with the recording as a `$binary` value. Audio is captured **locally** and never leaves your device — pair it with [Transcribe Audio](/nodes/builtin/ai/localai/transcribe-audio/) for a fully on-device voice-to-text step.

## What it does

When the run reaches this node it **suspends** and shows a recording popup — a live waveform and a timer that counts up to your **Max length**, with **Cancel**, **Restart**, and **Stop & send**. You record, then stop, and the captured audio flows out of the node as `$binary`. Like other interactive pauses (approvals, in-page forms), the run signals that it is waiting for you, and the popup reappears whenever you reopen the paused workflow.

## Recording on the page you're on (browser extension)

In the browser extension, when a run pauses at this node, a small **recording card** also appears on the web page you're currently viewing — so you can record without switching to the app. It moves through three steps:

1. **Waiting** — shows your Prompt and the maximum length. **Start recording** begins; **Skip** just closes the card.
2. **Recording** — a live waveform and a timer counting up to **Max length** (recording stops automatically there). **Cancel**, **Restart**, or **Stop**.
3. **Review** — play back the clip, then **Send & continue run** to resume the workflow with it, or **Re-record**.

The audio stays on your device: it goes straight from the page to your workflow. Because the card runs inside the page, the browser asks for **that site's** microphone permission the first time. If the site blocks the microphone or no microphone is found, the card says so — record from the recording popup in the app instead. Closing or skipping the card doesn't cancel anything: the run keeps waiting, and you can still record from the app.

## When to use it

Capture a voice note, a spoken command, or dictation as part of a workflow — then transcribe, summarize, or store it. Because it's a durable suspend, it fits anywhere you'd pause for human input.

## Outputs

{/* AUTO:outputs:start */}
One output port.

Each output item has the fields `recordedAt` (text), `$binary` (key-value pairs).
{/* AUTO:outputs:end */}

Resumes with the recording as a **`$binary`** value (an audio blob, e.g. `audio/webm`) plus a `recordedAt` timestamp. Feed the `$binary` straight into [Transcribe Audio](/nodes/builtin/ai/localai/transcribe-audio/) or any node that accepts binary media.

## Notes

- The microphone is accessed via `getUserMedia`; the browser asks for permission the first time (per site, for the in-page card).
- Audio is captured client-side and **never uploaded** by this node.
- If no audio is captured (the popup is dismissed), the node continues with an empty output.
