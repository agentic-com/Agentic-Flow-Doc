## Test it

<Steps>

1. Install the recipe and open **Your form values**. Put your own values in.
2. Open **Fill the form** and change the field ids to the ones on your form.
3. Save the workflow, open the page with the form, right-click it and choose **Agentic Workflow** › **Personal Workflows** › **Fill Form from JSON**.
4. Approve the request. The fields fill in and the form is sent.

</Steps>

## Variations

| Variation | What to change | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ask for one value each time | Add an [In-Page Form](/nodes/builtin/flow/in-page-form/) before Form Fill to type, say, an order number on the page. | Intermediate |
| Fill without sending | Remove Submit Form so you check the form before you send it. | Beginner |
| Click through several steps | Use [UI Automation](/nodes/extension/ui/automation/) to click **Next** and [Wait For Element](/nodes/extension/ui/wait-for-element/) between pages. | Advanced |

## Troubleshoot

- **Some fields stay empty.** Their ids differ from the ones in Form Fill. Use [Get Forms Schema](/nodes/extension/getformsschema/) to read the real ids.
- **The wrong form is filled.** The page has several forms. Change **formSelector** in Form Fill and the **DOM Selector** in Submit Form to target the right one, such as `#contact-form`.
- **The run seems stuck.** It's waiting for your approval. Approve it in the run panel or the in-page card.
