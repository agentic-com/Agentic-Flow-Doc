## How recipes work

Each recipe is a real workflow from the AWFlow marketplace, drawn step by step:

- **Install in AWFlow** sends the workflow to the extension, or opens the web app's import page. You review it before anything is saved.
- **You'll need** lists what the import leaves blank for you to fill in: a connection, or a model to download once.
- **It touches** lists the pages, sites, apps and models the workflow uses, worked out from its steps the same way as the permission list the marketplace shows before you install.
- **Make it yours** under each step names the setting to change, and **Variations** suggests bigger changes.

Recipes that need a [knowledge base](/app/knowledge-bases/overview/) of your own, such as chatting with your notes, are on the [Marketplace](https://app.awflow.io/#/app/marketplace) for now: a knowledge base can't travel inside a file. New to AWFlow? Start with the [quick intro](/get-started/quick-intro/).
