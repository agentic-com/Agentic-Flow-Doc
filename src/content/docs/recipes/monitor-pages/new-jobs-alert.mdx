## Test it

<Steps>

1. Install the recipe. In **Send a push notification**, pick a connection and set **Topic** to a hard-to-guess name such as `jobs-k7f3q`. See [Push Notification](/nodes/builtin/integration/push-notification/).
2. Subscribe to the same topic in the ntfy app on your phone.
3. In **Watch the job list**, pick the job list on the careers page with the crosshair, then save and activate the workflow.
4. Keep the page open. To test right away, edit the list's text in the browser's developer tools: your phone shows **New jobs**.

</Steps>

## Variations

| Variation | What to change | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alert in Slack or Discord | Replace Push Notification with [Slack](/nodes/builtin/integration/slack/) or [Discord](/nodes/builtin/integration/discord/). | Beginner |
| Alert on the computer only | Replace it with [Browser Notification](/nodes/extension/browsernotification/); no connection needed. | Beginner |
| Watch headlines or reviews | Point **CSS Selectors** at a news list or a reviews section instead. Several selectors can share one workflow. | Beginner |

## Troubleshoot

- **No alert.** Element Change only watches pages that are open. Keep the careers page in a tab, and check that the selector still matches after the site changes its layout.
- **Too many alerts.** Some pages rewrite the list on every scroll. Raise **Debounce (ms)**, or watch a more specific element such as the job count.
- **The phone gets nothing.** The topic on your phone differs from the one in the node; topics are case-sensitive.
