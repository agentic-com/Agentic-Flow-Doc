## In detail

### One word per thing

The app now uses one plain word for each thing, everywhere: a workflow is made of **steps** (the node reference keeps the word *node*), you press **Run** (and **Run step** for one step), past runs are under **Runs**, a workflow is **On** or **Off**, and the accounts you link are **Connections**. Steps and triggers have plain names too: *When I press Run*, *If*, *Route by value*, *Join branches*, *Keyboard shortcut*, *Test helper*… The old names still work in search. See the [glossary](/concepts/glossary/).

### Your saved workflows keep working

AWFlow 1.0 changes how some steps and some workflow rules behave, but **never for a workflow you already have**:

- each saved step keeps its **version**, and shows **Update available** when a newer one exists ([Step versions](/app/workflows/step-versions/));
- each workflow keeps its **behaviour** (Classic for workflows made before 1.0), and you can preview and upgrade it when you want ([Workflow behaviour versions](/app/workflows/behaviour-versions/)).

What you may still notice is listed in [Breaking changes](/releases/breaking-changes/#v100).

### Build faster

Map data by tapping a value, get suggestions and a live preview while typing an expression, open a full-screen expression editor, add steps from the keyboard, paste steps between workflows, tidy the canvas in one click, and keep values you reuse as [variables](/app/variables/). Step settings put the required fields first and say what the default is.

### Run, debug, measure

[Runs](/app/workflows/run-history/) records every run, keeps the data of failed runs, and lets you **Debug in editor** or **Run again**. [Test runs](/app/workflows/test-runs/) score an AI workflow on a dataset. [Insights](/app/insights/) show how often your workflows run, fail and save you time.

### Keep your data safe

[Health](/app/settings/health/) checks your setup, [backups](/app/settings/storage/#backups) keep an encrypted copy of everything on this device, drafts survive closing the panel, and two windows can no longer overwrite each other's changes. See also [Updating AWFlow](/app/updating-awflow/).
