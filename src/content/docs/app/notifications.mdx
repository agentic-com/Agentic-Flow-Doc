AWFlow tells you about things in two ways: the **bell** for account updates, and **waiting-run alerts** when a workflow needs your answer.

## The inbox

The inbox icon sits at the top right of the app. Since AWFlow 0.9 it gathers notifications **and** runs waiting for you, with one count. (The screenshot below shows the earlier bell.)

## What you're notified about

- your Marketplace listing (or agent template) was **approved**, needs **changes**, or was **suspended**,
- a workflow you installed was **taken down for safety**,
- someone wrote a **review** of your listing,
- a **report** you filed was resolved,
- someone **used** your workflow,
- a workflow you **requested** on the [request board](/app/request-board/) was published.

## The Notifications page

The page lists every notification, newest first. Unread ones are highlighted. Search them, and switch between **All** and **Unread** (both kept in the page address). Click one to mark it read and open what it is about. **Mark all read** clears the unread count.

## Runs waiting for you

When a workflow pauses for an approval or a form, the inbox counts it. Open it to approve, reject, or open the run; it lands on the workflow's **Runs** tab. You can also answer in the workflow's [run history](/app/workflows/run-history/#runs-that-need-you).

You are also alerted outside the app:

- **In the extension**, when AWFlow isn't the window in front of you, a desktop notification says *Your approval is needed*. It stays until you act.
- **When the app is in front of you** (or in the web app), a short chime plays. Turn it off with **Play a sound when your approval is needed** in [Preferences](/app/settings/preferences/#approval-sound).

### Notices from AWFlow

The inbox also shows short **notices from AWFlow** (a planned maintenance, a feature to try), marked *From AWFlow*. They count until you open the inbox; **×** hides one, with **Undo**. A notice that AWFlow needs an update can't be dismissed: it stays at the top, with **Update AWFlow**, and a banner says the same. See [Updating AWFlow](/app/updating-awflow/).

A form workflow you are filling in shows its next pages in place, with no badge, sound or notification while it's open.

## Newsletter

The newsletter is separate from notifications. Subscribe or unsubscribe in [Settings › General](/app/settings/general/#newsletter).
