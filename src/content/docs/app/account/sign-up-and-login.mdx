You don't need an account to use AWFlow. An account adds cloud storage, the Marketplace, publishing and notifications. See [Local or cloud](/app/account/local-vs-cloud/) to decide.

## Create an account with email

<Steps>

1. On the login screen, click **Sign up**.

2. Enter your **Name**, **Email**, a **Password** of at least 8 characters, and **Confirm Password**.

3. Click **Sign up**. You are taken back to the login screen.

4. Open the email we sent you and click the link. A page confirms *Your email has been verified*. Click **Login to your account**.

</Steps>

## Log in

Enter your **Email** and **Password** and click **Login**.

To use a social account instead, click the **GitHub**, **Google** or **Hugging Face** button under *Or continue with*:

- In the extension, a small window opens for you to finish signing in. Come back to AWFlow when you're done; it notices and logs you in. Click **Continue** if it doesn't.
- In the web app, the page goes to the provider and comes back.

The language picker in the top corner changes the language of these screens.

<Aside>
If you see **Please reset your password**, your account needs a new password before you can log in. Click **Email me a reset link** and follow [Reset your password](/app/account/reset-password/).
</Aside>

## Use AWFlow without an account

Click **Continue without an account** under the login form. Your workflows are saved in this browser. You can sign in later at any time.

## Log out

Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and click **Log out**. AWFlow switches to a local workspace on this device: your local workflows stay usable; cloud items come back when you log in again.
