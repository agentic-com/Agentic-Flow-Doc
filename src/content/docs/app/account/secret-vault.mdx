Keys and passwords you save in this browser are locked with a **passphrase** that only you know. The key is made from your passphrase, it is never saved anywhere, and your secrets can only be read after you unlock them.

**A passphrase is required before you save a key on this device.** The first time you save one, AWFlow asks you to set it, then finishes the save. If you cancel, nothing is saved.

It protects:

- every credential in a local workspace, and local credentials when you're signed in,
- API keys of AI providers (**Settings › Providers**) and header values of observability collectors,
- your conversations and remembered facts (memory). Titles, sizes and dates stay readable so lists still work.

Cloud credentials are not covered; they are encrypted on the AWFlow server. See [Privacy & data](/app/privacy-and-data/#connections-and-secrets).

<Aside type="danger">
There is no recovery. If you forget the passphrase, the protected secrets can't be decrypted by anyone.
</Aside>

## Set your passphrase

The easiest way is to save a key: **Protect your keys with a passphrase** opens by itself. Type a **Passphrase** of at least 8 characters, repeat it in **Confirm passphrase**, and click **Protect my keys**. Your key is then saved.

You can also set it ahead of time:

<Steps>

1. Open **Settings › Preferences › Local secret protection**.

2. Type a **Passphrase** of at least 8 characters and repeat it in **Confirm passphrase**.

3. Click **Turn on protection**. Your saved keys are re-encrypted, and your memory is encrypted in the background.

</Steps>

### Keys saved before passphrases were required

Keys you saved earlier keep working. Until you set a passphrase, the app shows **Set a passphrase to protect your N saved keys**. Click **Set a passphrase** to protect them now, or **Not now** to hide the message until you next open the browser.

Moving connections to this device and restoring a backup that contains keys also ask for the passphrase first.

## Unlock and lock

The vault locks every time the app reloads. While it is locked:

- the Credentials page and the workflow setup panel ask for your passphrase,
- memory can't be read, searched or added to,
- you can't save new local credentials.

Type your passphrase and click **Unlock**. It stays unlocked for this session. Click **Lock now** to lock it early.

## Change or remove the passphrase

When unlocked:

- **Change passphrase** asks for the current one and a new one.
- **Disable protection** is only offered when no key is saved on this device. Protection stays on while keys are saved: to turn it off, delete them first. Disabling decrypts your memory, and you'll need to set a passphrase again before you save another key.
