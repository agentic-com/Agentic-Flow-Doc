**In the app:** open your workflow, agent or team in the **Marketplace** › **Performance** tab. To share stats as an installer: the **Install** dialog, or **Settings › Privacy › Help marketplace authors**.

When you share a workflow, an agent or a team on the marketplace, you can't see how it does on other people's devices: whether it runs, how often it fails, and why. With this feature, people who install it can choose to send **anonymous daily stats** about it. You then see the result on a private **Performance** tab of its Marketplace page, once enough installs share stats that no single person can be singled out.

- [For authors: the Performance tab](#for-authors-the-performance-tab)
- [For installers: what you share](#for-installers-what-you-share)

## For authors: the Performance tab

Open the Marketplace page of something you published: from **Marketplace**, or from **My published workflows** for a workflow. Above the page you see two tabs: **Marketplace page** (what everyone sees) and **Performance**. Only you see the **Performance** tab. Nobody else, installers included, sees these numbers.

You need to be signed in with the account that published it.

### How the numbers are kept anonymous

These rules apply to every number on the tab:

- **Nothing shows below 10 installs.** Until 10 installs share stats in the chosen period, the tab shows **Not enough installs yet**, with how many have shared so far (for example **6 of 10**), a progress bar and the total number of installs. A longer range often gets you there sooner.
- **Small counts read "< 5".** A count from 1 to 4 shows as **< 5** instead of the exact number. Error types or 👎 reasons with fewer than 5 runs each are grouped into one line, **Other (each under 5)**.
- **Percentages are rounded** to whole numbers.
- **Hidden for this filter.** A view can have 10 installs or more and still be held back, when a smaller group inside it (a version, or the changed or unchanged copies) has fewer than 10. The tab then says **Hidden for this filter** and offers **All versions** or **All installs**.
- **The automatic switch to all installs.** When the **Unmodified** view of a workflow is held back this way, the tab switches to **All installs** on its own, with a note: *Showing all installs: the unmodified view is hidden while some groups have fewer than 10 installs.* Choose **Unmodified** again at any time; it shows **Hidden for this filter** until the groups are large enough.

<Aside type="note" title="Where the numbers come from">
Only installs whose owner chose to share send stats, once a day, for the days before today. An install that has AWFlow closed for a few days sends them when it opens again (up to 7 days back), so the last days of a range can still grow. Stats are kept 13 months. Which step fails and how people change a workflow come in a later update.
</Aside>

## For installers: what you share

Sharing stats is **off unless you say yes**, item by item. It is separate from AWFlow's [usage statistics](/app/privacy-and-data/#usage-statistics-and-error-reports), and it needs an account: nothing is sent in local mode.

### When you install

The **Install** dialog of a marketplace workflow has a checkbox **Help \{author\} improve \{item\}**, for example *Help Maya Chen improve Daily news digest*. It starts **unticked**. An agent or a team has the same checkbox in **What it can do**, above **Install**.

Only counts and fixed categories are sent, never text. The anonymous ID is different for each item, so two items you share can't be linked to each other or to you. It comes from a random secret kept on this device, separate from the usage statistics install ID: **Reset install ID** doesn't change it.

**Never share** keeps your switches as they are: choose **Ask me each time** again and the items that were on start sharing again. Stats resume from the next day: nothing from the days sharing was off, or from the day you turned it back on, is ever sent. The same goes for an item you switch back on.

You can also turn sharing off for one item where you use it:

- **An agent:** its **Monitor** tab has a **Sharing stats with \{author\}** switch.
- **A workflow:** in the workflow's **Settings**.
