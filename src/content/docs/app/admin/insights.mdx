**Insights** (Admin panel › Marketplace › **Insights**) shows how people find, install and run Marketplace workflows. Pick **7 days**, **30 days** or **90 days** at the top.

<Aside>
Counts only include people who share anonymous usage statistics, and each person counts once per listing and step. Read the numbers as trends, not totals.
</Aside>

## The cards

- **Install funnel**: from opening a listing to the first run, through *Listing viewed*, *Install clicked*, *Permissions allowed*, *Installed*, *Setup finished* and *Ran once*. Each step shows what share of the previous step made it.
- **Declined at permissions**: how often people said no after seeing what a workflow can do, broken down by capability such as *Runs code*, *Internet access* or *Connected accounts*.
- **Where installs come from**: *Search*, *Home rails*, *Shared links*, *Request board* or *Other*, plus top campaigns when there are any.
- **Activity**: searches (and how many found nothing), publishing, approvals and rejections, reviews, reports, requests and votes, and install errors.
- **Listings**: each listing's views, install rate, declined permissions, first runs and rating, most viewed first.

Values in red are worth a look. Hover them for the reason: few views turning into installs, many people declining the permissions, or few installs ever running.
