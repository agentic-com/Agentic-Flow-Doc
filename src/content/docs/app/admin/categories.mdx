**Categories** (Admin panel › **Categories**) controls the workflow categories people browse on the Marketplace.

The list shows each category's icon, label and description, its **Slug**, the number of **Workflows**, its **Order**, **Tags** and **Color**. Search by label or filter by tag.

## Create a category

<Steps>

1. Click **New category**.

2. Pick an **Icon** (an emoji), type a **Label**, and optionally a **Description**, **Color**, **Sort order** and comma-separated **Tags**.

3. The slug is filled in from the label. Click **Edit** next to it if you want a different one. You can't change it later.

4. Click **Create category** (or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd>).

</Steps>

The new category's page opens. If the slug is already taken, you are told so.

## Edit a category

Click a category, or use **⋮ › Edit**. The **Details** tab lets you change everything except the slug. Click **Save changes**, or **Reset** to undo your edits.

## Choose its workflows

Open the **Workflows** tab (or **⋮ › Manage workflows**):

- **Add workflows** opens a search of published workflows. Tick the ones you want and click **Add workflows**.
- The **X** on a row removes that workflow from the category straight away.

## Delete a category

Deleting removes the category from the Marketplace and unlinks its workflows. The workflows themselves are not deleted.

- From the list, **⋮ › Delete** asks you to confirm.
- From the category page, **Danger zone › Delete category** asks you to type the slug first.

<Aside type="caution">
Deleting can't be undone. Workflows that were only in this category won't appear under any category until you add them elsewhere.
</Aside>
