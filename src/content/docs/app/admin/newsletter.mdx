**Newsletter** (Admin panel › **Newsletter**) is where you send product news to members who subscribed. The header shows how many subscribers and issues there are. For how members subscribe, see [Newsletter](/app/newsletter/).

## Write and send an issue

<Steps>

1. Click **New issue**. A draft is created and the editor opens above the list.

2. Fill in the **Subject** and the **Body**. The body uses Markdown. Type `{{name}}` where you want the recipient's name.

3. Click **Send test** to email the issue to yourself first. It saves the draft too.

4. When it looks right, click **Send now**. A confirmation shows how many members will get it. Click **Send to N**.

</Steps>

**Save draft** keeps your work without sending. **Send now** stays disabled until both the subject and the body are filled in.

<Aside type="caution">
Sending can't be undone. The issue goes to every subscribed member; there is no way to pick a smaller audience.
</Aside>

## The issues list

The table shows each issue's **Subject**, **Status** (*draft*, *sending* or *sent*), **Recipients** and **Sent** date. It is your sending history.

Drafts have a **⋮** menu with **Edit** and **Delete**. Delete removes the draft immediately, without asking. Sent issues can't be changed.
