**Agent templates** (Admin panel › Marketplace › **Agent templates**) is the review queue for agents and teams that people share to the Marketplace. For what users see, read [Agent & team templates](/app/chat-and-agents/templates/).

## Pick a queue

Switch between **pending**, **approved**, **rejected**, **suspended** and **retired**.

Each card shows the template's kind, members, name, author, how many agents it has and how many workflows it needs, and its description. An **Update in review** label means a live template has a new version waiting; a summary lists what changed. The live version stays up until you approve the update.

Click **Preview** to read each agent's name, role and instructions before deciding.

## Decide

Type a note in **Note to the author** when needed, then:

- **Approve** (or **Approve update**) publishes it.
- **Reject** (pending queue only) sends it back. A note is required.
- **Suspend** (approved queue only) takes a live template down. A note is required.

The author sees your note. Every decision is recorded in the [Audit log](/app/admin/audit-log/).
