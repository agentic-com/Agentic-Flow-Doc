A **connection** (a *credential* in earlier versions) is a saved key, token or sign-in that lets a step act in another service: an API key, a Slack bot token, a Google sign-in. You add it once, then pick it in any step. When you share or export a workflow, your connections stay behind.

<Callout type="tip" title="Looking for one app?" href="/app/connections/" linkLabel="All connections">

Each service has its own page with the exact steps, the permissions to grant and the common errors: Slack, Notion, Google, OpenAI and more.

</Callout>

## From the Connections page

Open **Connections** in the sidebar (under **Your data**) and press **Add connection**. Everything else happens in the **New connection** form. In the side panel the form comes in two steps: first the list of apps, then the rest.

For an app, saving also **tests** it (**Save and test**): AWFlow makes one read-only request (for example "who am I" on GitHub or Slack) and shows **Connected as …** with the account name, or **Not working** / **Missing permission** with the reason. The account name stays on this device.

## From a step

A step that needs an app has a **Connection** field that lists only that app's connections. With none yet, press **Connect Slack** (or the app's name): the form opens in a sheet over the step, with the app already chosen. When you save, the connection is picked in the step and tested. With exactly one connection for the app, a new step picks it for you.

In **Ready to run**, **Connect** on a group ("Connect Google Sheets · used in 3 steps") connects every step of the group at once.

Pasting a `curl` command into an [HTTP Request](/nodes/builtin/core/http-request/) step (**Paste cURL**) turns the key it contains into a connection draft, ready to save.

## Types

| Type | Fields | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bearer token** | Bearer Token | Most app tokens and API keys (Slack, Notion, GitHub…). Paste only the token: AWFlow adds `Bearer`. |
| **Basic (username + password)** | Username, Password | Services that take two values, such as Trello (API key + token) or Pushover. An empty password is fine, for keys like Stripe's. |
| **API key in a header** | Header name, Key | APIs whose docs ask for a header such as `X-API-Key`. The form previews what each request will send. |
| **API key in the URL** | Parameter name, Key | APIs that take the key in the address, such as `?api_key=…`. |
| **JWT** | JWT | A value sent exactly as typed. |
| **Sign in with the app (OAuth2)** | Client ID, Client Secret (optional) | Google (Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Calendar) and Microsoft 365, with [your own OAuth client](/app/connections/oauth-client/). |

The two **API key** types are rolling out: they are offered for connections kept on this device, and for cloud connections once your account has them. A saved connection keeps its type.

<Callout type="info" title="Client Secret">

Microsoft 365 is a **public client**: it has no secret, so the field is hidden and any old value is cleared when you save. A Google **Web application** client needs its secret; a Google **Desktop app** client has none.

</Callout>

## Allowed sites

A connection can say **where its key may be sent**. **Allowed sites** lists the sites (for example `api.github.com`, or `*.example.com`). AWFlow then refuses to send the key anywhere else, even if a step points elsewhere, and never follows a redirect with it to another site. A new app connection starts with its app's sites; empty means any site.

On the Connections page, a connection without sites shows **Any site** and can offer **Lock to** a site from the sites your workflows actually call.

## Where connections are stored

- **On this device**: encrypted in this browser. The key sits on the same device, which guards against casual inspection. Turn on the [local secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/) to lock local connections with a passphrase.
- **In your account (cloud)**: stored encrypted on your AWFlow account, so you can use them on your other devices.

If you use AWFlow without an account, connections are always kept on this device. See [Privacy & your data](/app/privacy-and-data/#connections-and-secrets).

## Tips

- Create **one connection per account and purpose**, and reuse it across workflows. Updating it updates every workflow that uses it.
- Give a token **only the permissions** your workflow needs. Each connection page lists them per operation.
- Never paste a secret into a step's normal fields: those travel with the workflow when you share it. [Health](/app/settings/health/) finds keys pasted into steps.
- Two Google (or Microsoft) connections for two accounts work side by side: each keeps its own sign-in.
