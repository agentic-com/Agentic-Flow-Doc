## Set it up

<Steps>

1. **Create the bot.** In Telegram, open a chat with **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`. Choose a name and a username ending in `bot`.
2. **Copy the token** BotFather replies with, like `123456789:ABCdefGhIJKlmNoPQRstuVWxyz`.
3. **Save it in AWFlow.** Open **Credentials** and click **Add Credential** (or **+ Add Credential** in a node's **Credential** field), then fill the **New Credential** form. In the browser side panel the form comes in two steps: tap **Telegram** in the list, then fill the rest.

4. **Start the bot.** Open a chat with your bot and press **Start**, or add it to the group or channel it should post in. A bot can't message someone who never started it.

</Steps>

<Callout type="tip" title="Check it's connected">

Run **Message › Send message** with your **Chat ID** and the text *Hello*: the message arrives in Telegram.

</Callout>

## Where your token lives

<Callout type="credential" title="Where your token lives" href="/app/privacy-and-data/#credentials-and-secrets" linkLabel="Privacy & your data">

The credential is stored where you choose when you create it:

- **Local (this browser only)**: encrypted in this browser. The encryption key is kept on the same device, so turn on the [local secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/) to lock it with a passphrase only you know.
- **Cloud (synced to your account)**: sent over an encrypted connection and stored encrypted on AWFlow's server, so it works on your other devices.

When a step runs, your browser sends it only to `api.telegram.org`. AWFlow's servers never relay the call.

</Callout>

## Common issues

- **"Unauthorized".** The token is wrong or was revoked. Get the current one from @BotFather (`/token`) and update the credential.
- **"Bad Request: chat not found".** The **Chat ID** is wrong, or the bot was never started in that chat or added to the group.
- **"Forbidden: bot was blocked by the user".** The person blocked the bot. They need to unblock it and press **Start**.
- **The token leaked.** Send `/revoke` to @BotFather and update the credential with the new token.
