Edit a credential when a token expires or is replaced, a password changes, or you want a clearer name. Every node and workflow that uses it picks up the change: you don't re-select it anywhere.

## Edit a credential

<Steps>

1. Open **Credentials** in the sidebar.
2. On the credential's row, open the **⋯** menu and click **Open**. The **Edit Credential** panel opens.
3. Change what you need: the name, the description, or the secret fields (token, username and password, Client ID).
4. For a Google or Microsoft 365 credential, click **Check connection** again. This signs in fresh, so newly added permissions are granted.
5. Click **Update Credential**.

</Steps>

## Move it between this browser and your account

In the same **⋯** menu, **Move to cloud** stores the credential encrypted on your AWFlow account, so you can use it on your other devices. **Move to local** brings it back into this browser only. See [Local or cloud](/app/account/local-vs-cloud/).

## Delete a credential

Choose **Delete** in the **⋯** menu and confirm. Workflows that used it stop at that step until you pick another credential.

## If it still fails after editing

- Run one step that uses it with **Test step**: the error names the problem, for example a missing scope.
- Check the service's own dashboard: the token may be revoked, or the account over its rate limit.
- Open the service's [connection page](/app/connections/) for the list of common errors.
