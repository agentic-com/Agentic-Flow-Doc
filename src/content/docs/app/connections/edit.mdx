Edit a connection when a token expires or is replaced, a password changes, or you want a clearer name. Every node and workflow that uses it picks up the change: you don't re-select it anywhere.

## Edit a connection

Open **Connections** in the sidebar. On the connection's row, open the **⋯** menu and click **Open**: the **Edit connection** form opens. Hover or tap a step to find it on the screen.

## Test it

Each connection shows its status from the last test: **Connected as …**, **Not working** (the key was refused) or **Missing permission**, with when it was last tested. **Test** runs the check again; saving the form tests it too. For a Google or Microsoft 365 connection, **Check connection** shows the account without asking you to consent again.

## Move it between this browser and your account

In the same **⋯** menu, **Move to cloud** stores the connection encrypted on your AWFlow account, so you can use it on your other devices. **Move to local** brings it back into this browser only. **Undo** in the toast moves it back.

To move several at once, tick them in the list and use **To cloud** or **To local** in the toolbar at the bottom: it shows the progress and how many were moved, skipped or failed. A move copies the connection, checks the copy, then deletes the original. See [Local or cloud](/app/account/local-vs-cloud/).

## Delete a connection

Choose **Delete** in the **⋯** menu. The confirmation lists the workflows that use it ("Checking which workflows use it…"): their steps stop working until you add a new connection. This can't be undone.

## If it still fails after editing

- Run one step that uses it with **Run step**: the error names the problem, for example a missing scope.
- Check the service's own dashboard: the token may be revoked, or the account over its rate limit.
- Open the service's [connection page](/app/connections/) for the list of common errors.
