A **connection** (a *credential* in the app) is a saved key, token or sign-in that lets a node or an agent act in another service. You create it once in **Credentials**, then pick it in any node that needs it. Each page below takes you from zero to a working connection.

## No connection needed

- **On-device AI**: models from [Local AI](/app/local-models/), Chrome's built-in AI and the Local AI nodes run in your browser with no key.
- **Ollama** on your computer: Aria and agents find running models by themselves, and the [Ollama](/nodes/builtin/ai/aidependencies/llm/ollama/) node only needs its address.
- **Browser steps** (read a page, click, fill a form, take a screenshot) use the tabs you are already signed in to.

<Callout type="credential" title="Where your tokens live" href="/app/privacy-and-data/#credentials-and-secrets" linkLabel="Privacy & your data">

Each credential is stored **locally** (encrypted in this browser, optionally behind your [secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/) passphrase) or **in the cloud** (encrypted on your AWFlow account). Calls go from your browser straight to the service. Workflow exports and shared workflows never include your secrets.

</Callout>
