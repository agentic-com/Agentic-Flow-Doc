**In the app:** sidebar › **Observability** › **Monitor** (`#/app/observability/monitor`), and the **Monitor** tab under **Observe** on an agent's page

**Monitor** shows how your AI runs are doing over days and weeks. Each run of Aria, your agents, teams, routines and AI workflow steps adds to its charts: how many runs finished, how many failed, how long they took and which tools they used. Everything is computed on this device from your [traces](/app/observability/traces/).

Monitor has two parts, picked at the top: **Dashboards** (this page) and **Alerts** (see [Alerts & budgets](/app/observability/alerts-and-budgets/)).

## The Overview dashboard

The **Overview** is the prebuilt dashboard. It opens first and needs no setup.

When you have [token budgets](/app/observability/alerts-and-budgets/#token-budgets), a **Token budgets** section sits at the top, with one meter per budget for this month. Below it the Overview has three sections.

**Traffic & errors**

| Tile or chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Finished traces** | Runs that ended, successfully or not. Runs still going or paused don't count. |
| **Error rate** | The share of finished traces that failed. |
| **Failed traces** | How many finished traces failed. |
| **Active agents** | How many agents ran. With one agent picked in the filters, this tile shows **Tool calls** instead. |
| **Traces per day** | Succeeded and failed runs, day by day. |
| **Top error types** | Failed traces by cause. |
| **Error rate by source** | The error rate of each source over time. |
| **Failed traces by agent** | Which agents failed most. |

**Latency**

| Chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **End-to-end latency** | How long a run takes, P50 and P99, in seconds. |
| **Time to first token** | How soon each model starts replying (P50 by model). |
| **Latency by model** | A table per model: where it **Runs on** (**Cloud** or **On device**), **Calls**, **P50**, **P99**, **TTFT P50** and **Errors**. |

**Tools & skills**

| Chart | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Tools** | Calls, failure rate and speed per tool. |
| **Rule decisions** | What your rules did with tool calls: allowed, asked or blocked. |

P50 is the typical run: half of the runs were faster. P99 is the slow end: only 1 run in 100 was slower.

## Range and filters

The filter row sits above the charts:

- **Range**: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days** (the default), **Last 30 days** or **Last 90 days**. Tiles compare the range with the period just before it.
- **Source**: **All**, **Agent**, **Aria**, **Team**, **Workflow step** or **Routine**.
- **Agent**: one of your agents. Agent and Source can't both be set: picking one clears the other.
- **Model**: one model. It applies to the model charts only, and the page says so.

A chart that can't follow a filter says so in its subtitle, for example *· All agents*. Its traces link then keeps only the filters it follows.

## Read a chart as a table

Every chart card has two buttons in its corner:

- **Table view** shows the same numbers as a table, for exact values or a screen reader. Click **Chart view** to go back.
- **Open matching traces** opens [Traces](/app/observability/traces/) with the same range and filters, so you can see the runs behind a number.

### The chart editor

| Field | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Data** | **Traces** (one per run), **Spans** (each model call, tool call and step inside a run) or **Feedback** (your 👍 / 👎). |
| **Metric** | **Count**, **Latency**, **Time to first token**, **Tokens**, **Error rate (ratio)** or **Feedback score**. |
| **Aggregation** | **Sum**, **Average**, **P50** or **P99**; under **More**: **P90**, **Min** and **Max**. |
| **Tokens** | For the Tokens metric: **Total**, **Input**, **Output** or **Cloud only**. |
| **Filters** | Click **Filter**, then pick a **Field**, an **Operator** and a **Value**, and click **Add filter**. Fields include **Agent**, **Source**, **Model**, **Tool**, **Runs on**, **Tag**, **Status**, **Feedback**, **Duration** and **Tokens**. |
| Filter scope | **Span** (the span itself), **Trace** (the run's first span) or **Whole tree** (any span in the run). |
| **Group by** | **Tool name**, **Agent**, **Model**, **Source**, **Runs on (cloud / on device)**, **Tag**, **Agent version**, **Attribute… (e.g. awf.surface)** or **None**. |
| **Chart** | **Line**, **Stacked**, **KPI**, **Ranked**, **Donut** or **Table**. |

With **Data** set to **Spans**, add a **Kind** filter (for example kind = LLM for model calls or TOOL for tool calls). Without one, the editor shows **Add a span kind filter** and won't save. Grouping or filtering by an attribute also needs **Spans** with a kind filter.

### Compare agent versions

Group by **Agent version** to see whether a new version of an agent does better than the last one: errors, latency or tokens per version, side by side. Pick the agent in the dashboard's **Agent** filter so only its versions show. Aria isn't versioned, so its runs fall in no group. Runs from [Evals](/app/observability/evals/) experiments never count here, so a test variant never counts as a real version. See [Agent versions](/app/observability/agent-versions/).

### Manage dashboards

- **Switch** with the dashboard picker next to **Dashboards**. The Overview is marked **Prebuilt**, your own dashboards **Custom**.
- **Rename** or **Delete** a dashboard from its ⋯ menu in the picker. Deleting removes its charts from this device; your traces stay.
- A custom dashboard has its own range and filters, saved with it, plus a **Group by** that applies to every chart without a Group by of its own (KPI charts excepted).
- **Edit a chart** with its pencil button. Its **Chart actions** menu has **Move up**, **Move down** and **Remove**. You can also drag a chart by its handle to reorder it.

## An agent's Monitor tab

On an agent's page, the **Monitor** tab under **Observe** shows the same kind of numbers for that agent only: **Runs**, **Errors**, **P50 latency**, **Cloud tokens** and **Helpful** (the share of 👍 ratings), then **Runs per day**, **Latency** and **Tools used**. It also holds the agent's [token budget](/app/observability/alerts-and-budgets/#token-budgets). Pick the range at the top, or click **Open in Observability** for the full Monitor filtered to this agent.

## What Monitor counts

- Runs of Aria, your agents, teams, routines and AI workflow steps.
- **Evals and Insights runs never count.** Experiments from [Evals](/app/observability/evals/) and analyses from [Insights](/app/observability/insights/) are your own test and analysis traffic, so they never move the charts, tiles or alerts. You still find them in Traces.
- A day with no runs is a gap in a line chart, not a zero.
- If **Recording** is off in **Settings › Observability**, new runs don't reach the charts, and Monitor says so. Token budgets still count. See [Settings & privacy](/app/observability/settings/).

<Aside type="note" title="For developers">
The Overview reads daily rollups, which are kept for 400 days even after the traces themselves are removed. Custom charts use the cheapest source that can answer them: the daily rollups, then a scan of the trace summaries (up to 20,000), then a scan of spans (up to 50,000, and only with a `kind` filter). Average, minimum and maximum latency always scan summaries. A scanned chart only sees traces still kept on this device and says so (*Traces kept on this device*); a scan that hit its limit is marked *Partial: scan limit reached*. When several dashboard filters apply, Agent wins over Source, and Source over Model.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- Monitor counts tokens, never money.
- Numbers come from this device only. Another browser or device has its own Monitor.
- With no runs in the range, Monitor says *No AI runs in this range yet.*; with filters set, **Clear filters** brings everything back.
- Error rates only count finished runs: a run that is still going or waiting for you is left out.
