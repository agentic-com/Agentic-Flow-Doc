**In the app:** sidebar › **Observability** (`#/app/observability`), and the **Observe** tabs on each agent's page

**Observability** records every run of Aria, your agents, teams, routines and AI workflow steps on this device. Use it to see why a reply was slow or wrong, to check that an agent stays within its token budget, to test a change before you keep it, and to learn what people ask your agents.

## The four tabs

The **Observability** page has four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Traces** | Every run, step by step: what the agent was asked, which models and tools it used, how long each step took, how many tokens it used, and what went wrong. |
| **Monitor** | Dashboards of runs, errors, response time and tokens over time, plus alerts and token budgets. |
| **Evals** | Datasets of test examples and experiments that compare versions of an agent before you keep a change. |
| **Insights** | Reports that group conversations into categories: what people ask, where an agent gets stuck, what it can't do yet. |

They build on each other. Your 👍 and 👎 on replies become test examples in Evals, Insights can turn a category into a dataset or a fix, and an experiment that wins becomes the agent's new version. [Observability concepts](/concepts/ai/observability/) explains how.

[Online evals](/app/observability/online-evals/) score a sample of an agent's real conversations as they happen. Set them up from the **Online evals** card on the agent's **Evals** tab; the scores then show on traces, in Monitor and in alerts.

## Where to find it

- **Everything:** in the sidebar, under **Automate**, click **Observability**. In the side panel, open **More**, then **Observability**.
- **One agent:** open the agent from **Agents & teams**. Next to its Build tabs, the **Observe** group has **Traces**, **Monitor**, **Evals** and **Insights** for that agent only. The agent's token budget is on its **Monitor** tab.
- **One reply:** under a reply in any chat, click **View trace** to open its trace. 👍 and 👎 next to it rate the reply.
- **Settings:** **Settings › Observability** controls what is recorded, how long it's kept and where copies are sent.

## Two ways to read a trace

By default, each trace reads like a short story: *Inbox Triage answered in 6.5 s using 14.1k tokens*, with plain step names such as *Thinking* or *Used gmail.search*. When a run failed, the story says why and offers a fix, such as **Reconnect Gmail**. (These examples are illustrative.)

Switch on **Developer view** on a trace to see the technical detail on the same data: span kinds, model ids, raw input and output, and the OpenTelemetry attributes. It is one setting (**Open traces in developer view** in [Settings & privacy](/app/observability/settings/)), so it stays on for every trace until you turn it off.

## Your data in brief

- Traces stay **in this browser**. Nothing is sent to the AWFlow servers.
- Prompts and replies are kept by default, because Evals and Insights need them. API keys, tokens and passwords are removed before saving.
- You can stop recording, keep timings and token counts only for chosen agents, and choose how long traces are kept.
- A copy leaves your device only if you add a collector of your own, such as Phoenix or Langfuse.
- Everything is measured in **tokens**. AWFlow never shows a price.

## Good to know

- Observability works the same for everyone, signed in or not. It needs no account.
- Only AI work is traced. For whole workflow runs, see [Run history](/app/workflows/run-history/) and [Workflow stats](/app/insights/).
- Experiments and Insights reports run their own traces. They never count in Monitor, but their tokens count toward the agent's budget.
- With recording off, budgets still count tokens, but no new traces are kept.
