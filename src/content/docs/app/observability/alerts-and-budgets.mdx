**In the app:** sidebar › **Observability** › **Monitor** › **Alerts** (`#/app/observability/monitor/alerts`); budgets on an agent's **Monitor** tab and a team's **Rules & limits** tab

**Alerts** watch your AI runs for you. You set a line, for example "Research Scout's error rate above 10% over the last hour", and AWFlow tells you when it is crossed and again when things are back to normal.

**Token budgets** cap how many tokens an agent or a team uses in a month. You are warned at 80%, and you choose what happens at 100%.

Both work without an account, and both count tokens only, never money.

### The condition

| Part | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Metric** | **Error rate** (%), **Latency P99** (seconds), **Tool failures** (a count), **Tokens** (cloud tokens) or **👎 feedback** (thumbs-down runs). |
| **Scope** | **All agents**, Aria or one agent; or a source (**Agent runs**, **Team runs**, **Workflow step runs**, **Routine runs**); or where the model runs (**Cloud models**, **On-device models**). |
| **Window** | The last 15 minutes, hour, 6 hours or 24 hours. |
| **Comparison** | **above**, **at least** or **below**. |
| **Threshold** | A number in the metric's unit. Each metric starts with a sensible value, for example 10% for error rate. |

For **Tool failures**, a **Tool** field appears: leave it empty for any tool, or type one, for example `slack.post_message`.

An error rate needs at least 5 finished runs in the window. With fewer, the alert waits.

## Fired and resolved

Each rule in the **Alert rules** list shows its state:

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **OK** | The value is on the right side of the line. |
| **Firing** | The line was crossed. You are told once, then not again until the **Don't repeat for** time has passed. |
| **Muted** | You muted the rule for 24 hours. It keeps its state but tells nobody. |

A firing alert **resolves by itself** once the value stays back under the line for 30 minutes (or above it, for a **below** rule). The resolve is an in-app notice only, and only when the fire was announced. If the runs stop altogether, a firing alert also resolves after 30 minutes.

The **Alerts** page also lists **Recent alerts**, fired and resolved, newest first, each with a **Traces** button that opens the runs behind it. Fired and resolved alerts are kept for 90 days.

### Where you see alerts

- **In-app:** a short message on the page you are on.
- **Desktop:** a system notification, with sound, as in the preview under **How it looks on your desktop**. Clicking it opens the alert. In the extension, AWFlow picks a badge, a chime or a system notification depending on whether you are looking at it. In the web app, ticking **Desktop notification (with sound)** asks the browser for permission, and no desktop notification is shown while the AWFlow tab has focus.
- **The bell:** an **Alerts** section lists the latest alerts, with **Mark all read** and **All alerts**. It works without an account. Only alerts that notified count as unread.
- **Monitor:** a red banner on the dashboards names the firing alert, for example *Research Scout error rate is 18.4%*, with **View alert** and **Open traces**. The **Alerts** button shows how many rules are firing.

### Mute, edit or delete a rule

Click a rule to open it. **Mute 24 h** silences it for a day (**Unmute** ends that early). Change any field and click **Save alert**. **Delete** removes the rule; its past alerts stay in **Recent alerts**.

## Token budgets

A budget is a monthly token cap for one agent (Aria included) or one team. It resets on the 1st of each month.

### Set a budget

1. Open the agent's page, then the **Monitor** tab under **Observe**. For a team, open its **Rules & limits** tab.
2. In **Monthly token budget**, type the cap, for example `2M` or `500k`. It saves as soon as you leave the field.
3. Next, keep **Cloud tokens only** (on-device models are free to run) or pick **All tokens (incl. on device)**.
4. Under **At 80%**, tick **In-app**, **Desktop** or both. The 100% notice uses the same channels.
5. Under **At 100%**, choose what happens (below).

Every change saves at once. To stop limiting, click **Remove budget** and confirm. Usage is still recorded; nothing is limited any more.

### The meter

Once a cap is set, a meter shows the tokens used this month against the cap, a dashed mark at 80% (**Warning at 80%**), and a hatched bar for where the month is heading (**Projected to** the last day of the month), based on the pace of the last 7 days. The same meters appear in the **Token budgets** section at the top of [Monitor](/app/observability/monitor/), fullest first, each with a link to manage it.

### At 80% and at 100%

At **80%**, you get one warning per month: *Research Scout has used 80% of its monthly token budget.* (an illustrative name). Runs carry on.

At **100%**, the agent does what you chose:

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Just notify me** | You get one notice. Runs continue on cloud models. |
| **Switch to on-device models** | Cloud calls stop and the agent keeps working on your installed local model. The notice names the model. If the on-device model fails to start, the run stops with an error: it never falls back to the cloud. If the agent is set to one cloud model, or no on-device model is installed, it keeps running in the cloud and the notice says so. |
| **Pause the agent** (or **Pause the team**) | New runs stop until next month or until you raise the budget. A run that hits the budget ends with *Research Scout reached its monthly token budget.* and a **Raise budget** button, which opens the budget. |

**Switch to on-device models** needs an installed model: without one, the option is greyed out with a link to **Local models**. See [Local models](/app/local-models/).

Raising the cap above the current usage re-arms the notices, so you are told again when the new line is crossed.

### Budgets in the Alerts list

Each budget also appears in the **Alert rules** list as a read-only rule named **Budget ·** and the agent's or team's name, marked **from budget**. Its condition reads like *Tokens ≥ 80% of 2M · month*. Clicking it opens the budget editor. Budget notices land in **Recent alerts** and the bell like any other alert.

## Evals and Insights runs

Runs started by [Evals](/app/observability/evals/) experiments and by [Insights](/app/observability/insights/) are analysis traffic. They never count in Monitor: they add nothing to the charts, and alerts ignore them.

Their tokens still count toward the agent's budget, because they are real model calls. The budget never pauses or switches an experiment run, so a comparison is never cut short or changed halfway. Before an experiment runs, its estimate shows the budget that remains.

<Aside type="note" title="For developers">
Alerts read the trace summaries of their window, not the daily rollups, so they can answer 15-minute windows and the hourly backtest. Each pass reads at most 10,000 summaries; when that cuts into a rule's window, the rule is skipped for that pass rather than under-counted. Rules are checked after new traces arrive (at most once a minute), every 5 minutes and once at start, in the app pages or the extension's background document, under a lock so only one checks at a time. Budgets are enforced by a budget middleware on every model call. Not budgeted yet: Aria's build-mode and summary calls, and AI workflow steps that run without an agent.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- Alerts are checked only while AWFlow is running; nothing is written while it is closed, so nothing can fire.
- The **Tokens** alert metric counts cloud tokens. Budgets count cloud tokens too, unless set to **All tokens (incl. on device)**. In both, input the model read from its cache counts for less.
- A rule needs a name; one saved without one reads *Untitled alert*.
- Teams used to have a cloud-token cap per thread. It is now a team budget with **Just notify me**; a cap you had changed from the default was moved over once.
- Alert rules, budgets and their history stay on this device.
