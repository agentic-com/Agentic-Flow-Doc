**In the app:** sidebar › **Observability** › **Traces** (`#/app/observability`), an agent's **Observe › Traces** tab, or **View trace** under any reply

A **trace** is the record of one AI run: what was asked, each model call, tool call and lookup, how long each took and how many tokens it used. Use traces to understand a slow or wrong reply, to find out why a run failed, and to rate replies so you can test fixes later.

## Find a run

Open **Observability**. The **Traces** tab lists every run of Aria, your agents, teams, routines and AI workflow steps on this device, newest first.

Narrow the list with the toolbar:

- **Search** looks through inputs, outputs and tool names.
- The date button picks **Last 24 hours**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **All time**.
- **Filter** opens more choices: **Status** (Succeeded, Failed, Running, Waiting, Interrupted), **Where it ran** (Anywhere, Cloud, On device), **Surface** (Page, Side panel, Notch, Quick chat, Try it, Routine, Workflow) and **Online score** (Any, **Low online score**, **Scored online**). Click **Show results** to apply them, or **Clear** to reset them. **Low online score** keeps the runs that an [online eval](/app/observability/online-evals/) scored low; it shows as a chip you can remove.
- In the **Spans** view, the chips pick a kind of step instead of a source: **Model calls**, **Tools**, **Lookups**, **Rules** or **Agents**.

The stats rail on the right follows your filters. Collapse it to make room, and bring it back with **Stats**. **Export** at the top downloads the runs shown.

The list loads more runs as you scroll. It shows up to the latest 1,000 runs; to see older ones, narrow the dates or filters.

## Read a trace

Open a run to see its trace. At the top, a short story says what happened, for example *Inbox Triage answered in 6.5 s using 14.1k tokens* (an illustrative example). When the run failed, the story says what went wrong and offers a fix.

Pick a view:

| View | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Conversation** | The whole conversation, turn by turn: your message, the reply, the tools it called, and the time and tokens of each turn. **Steps** on a turn opens that turn's steps. |
| **Steps** | The run as a tree of steps on the left and the selected step's details on the right. |
| **Timeline** | Each step as a bar on a time line, so you can see what took longest and what ran in parallel. Click a bar to open that step in **Steps**. |

### Steps

Steps have plain names: *Thinking* for a model call, *Used gmail.search* for a tool, *Recalled memories* or *Looked up knowledge* for a lookup, *Checked rule* for a rule decision, *Prepared context* for what the model received. A failed step is marked in red, and its details explain the error.

The details pane has these tabs:

- **Input & output** shows what the step received and returned. For a model call, it shows the messages; for a tool, its arguments and result. Switch between **JSON** and **Raw**, and copy the text.
- **Events** lists what happened during the step, such as the first token, an Auto model switch or an approval request.
- **Attributes** appears in Developer view. It lists every recorded attribute.

### Developer view

Turn on the **Developer view** switch in the trace's header (**Dev** on a narrow screen). Steps then show their raw names and kinds (`LLM`, `TOOL`, `RETRIEVER`…), the story shows the trace and session ids, and the details pane adds **Attributes**. The switch is the same setting as **Open traces in developer view** in [Settings & privacy](/app/observability/settings/), so it stays on for every trace until you turn it off.

### Other actions

- **Export trace** (under the story, or in the **More** menu) downloads this trace.
- **Copy trace ID** (in **More**) copies the id, for a bug report.
- On a narrow screen, **Copy link** copies the trace's address.

## Fix a failed run

When a run failed, the story names the step at fault and offers up to two buttons:

| Button | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Reconnect** and the app's name, for example **Reconnect Gmail** | A tool was refused because its connection expired. It opens your connections. |
| **Open AI providers** | A model refused the key, or its quota ran out, and no other model took over. It opens **Settings › AI providers**. |
| **Retry this turn** | For Aria and agent chats, when a run failed or was interrupted: it opens the conversation again so you can resend. |

A rule that blocked an action, or an approval you declined, is not treated as a failure. If the run went on to fail anyway, the story points at the real cause.

A run that stopped because the page was closed or reloaded shows as **Interrupted**. A run waiting for your approval shows as **Waiting**. When you answer, the rest of the run is added to the same trace.

## Rate a reply

Every reply from Aria and your agents has 👍 and 👎 buttons: under the reply in each chat, and in the trace's header.

1. Click 👍 (**Helpful**) to save a positive rating at once.
2. Click 👎 (**Not helpful**) to open **What went wrong?**
3. Pick any reasons: **Wrong answer**, **Wrong tool**, **Missed context**, **Too slow**, **Unsafe action** or **Other**.
4. Optionally, add a comment in **Tell us more (optional)**.
5. Keep **Add this turn to the … regressions dataset** ticked to save the turn as a test example for that agent, for example *Inbox Triage · regressions*.
6. Click **Send feedback**.

Click the pressed thumb again to remove your rating. Your rating and reasons show under **Your feedback** in the trace's **Steps** view.

Feedback **stays on this device**. It feeds the helpful rate in Monitor, datasets in Evals and the summaries in Insights. A rated trace is kept even when older traces are cleaned up.

## Add a run to a dataset

To keep a reply as a test example without rating it, click **Add to dataset** under the trace's story. This shows on agent and Aria replies only.

1. Pick a **Dataset**, or **New dataset…** and type its name.
2. Optionally, write an **Expected answer**: what a good answer says. Leave it empty to score without a reference.
3. Click **Add to dataset**.

You can then replay the example against new versions of the agent. See [Evals](/app/observability/evals/).

## Online scores

When the agent has [online evals](/app/observability/online-evals/) and this run was in the sample, **Online scores** shows under the trace's story: one chip per evaluator, ✓ for a pass and ✕ for a low score, and which model scored it. Click a ✕ chip to read the judge's explanation, with **Add to dataset** and **Not right? Rate it yourself**. See [Scores on a trace](/app/observability/online-evals/#scores-on-a-trace).

## An agent's traces

On an agent's page, **Observe › Traces** shows that agent's runs only, with its numbers for the last 7 days above the list. **Open in Observability** opens the same list in the full **Traces** tab, filtered to the agent.

<Aside type="note" title="For developers">
Traces follow the OpenTelemetry model. Each span has an OpenInference kind (`openinference.span.kind`: `AGENT`, `CHAIN`, `LLM`, `TOOL`, `RETRIEVER`, `EMBEDDING`, `RERANKER`, `GUARDRAIL`, `PROMPT`, `EVALUATOR`) plus `gen_ai.*` and `awf.*` attributes. Trace ids are 32 hex characters and span ids 16. A conversation's id is the `session.id`. Feedback is stored as an annotation on the root span. Developer view shows all of this; **Export trace** gives you the raw data. See [Observability concepts](/concepts/ai/observability/).
</Aside>

## Good to know

- With **Record traces** off, the list says **Recording is off** and offers **Turn on recording**.
- A trace that's no longer on this device (deleted, or older than your retention setting) opens as **This trace is no longer on this device**.
- The stats are based on the latest 2,000 runs. When a search finds nothing in the most recent runs, **Keep searching** looks further back.
- Tokens are counted; AWFlow never shows a price.
