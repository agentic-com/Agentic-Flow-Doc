**In the app:** **Settings › Observability** (`#/app/settings/observability`), under **Safety & privacy**

**Settings › Observability** controls what AWFlow records about AI runs, how long it keeps it, and where it sends it. Everything on this page applies to this browser.

## Recording

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Record traces** | On | Records every turn of Aria, agents, teams, routines and AI workflow steps. Off: no new traces are kept. |
| **Include prompts and replies** | On | Keeps what was asked, what the model received and what it answered. Evals and Insights need it. Only available while **Record traces** is on. |
| **Don't record content for…** | None | Agents you add here keep timing and token counts only, even with content on. Use it for agents that handle sensitive data. |
| **Open traces in developer view** | Off | Opens every trace in [Developer view](/app/observability/traces/#developer-view): span kinds, model ids and OpenTelemetry attributes. |

**Open traces** at the top of the card takes you to the trace list.

### Keep content out for one agent

1. Next to **Don't record content for…**, click **Add**.
2. Pick the agent.
3. To record its content again, click the **×** on its chip.

The agent's traces still show each step, its time and its tokens, but no messages, tool inputs or results. Traces recorded before the change keep what they already hold.

## What is removed before saving

With **Include prompts and replies** on, AWFlow cleans the content before it is stored or sent anywhere:

- **Secrets in text.** API keys, `Bearer` tokens, passwords and `key=`, `token=` or `secret=` values in links are removed.
- **Keys AWFlow knows.** The values of your connections and AI provider keys are replaced by a `[secret:…]` placeholder that names the secret, wherever they appear.
- **Images and files.** Images embedded in the text become `[image]` and other files `[file]`. Their contents are never stored.

Removing secrets works on patterns and on the keys AWFlow holds. It can't recognize every piece of personal information. If an agent handles data you don't want recorded, add it to **Don't record content for…**.

## Storage on this device

Traces live in this browser only. The meter shows an estimate of the space used, split into **Traces**, **Datasets & experiments** and **Insights**.

| Setting | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep traces for** | 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, Until full | 30 days |
| **Max size** | 100 MB, 200 MB, 500 MB, 1.0 GB | 200 MB |

When traces get older than **Keep traces for**, or the store goes over **Max size**, AWFlow deletes the oldest ones a little at a time, in the background. It keeps any trace that is **pinned**: used by a dataset, rated with 👍 or 👎, or part of an Insights report. Runs that are still running or waiting for you are kept too, for up to 30 days.

Datasets and experiment results are never deleted automatically.

### Export or clear

- **Export (OTLP JSON)** downloads your traces as a standard OpenTelemetry file that other tools can import. A very large store exports the latest traces, and AWFlow tells you how many.
- **Clear traces** deletes every trace on this device. Confirm with **Delete traces**. Pinned traces are deleted too. Datasets, experiments and insights stay.

## What never leaves your device

- Traces, datasets, experiments and Insights reports are **never sent to the AWFlow servers**. Observability works without an account.
- A copy is sent only to a **collector you add yourself**, and only from then on. You choose per collector whether it gets content or **Timing and tokens only**. See [Send to your tools](/app/observability/collectors/).
- **Anonymous usage statistics** are separate. They are sent only while **Share anonymous usage statistics** is on in [Settings › Privacy](/app/settings/privacy/), and they hold counts and event names, never your text or trace ids.
- Your 👍 and 👎 feedback stays on this device.

## Tokens only

Observability counts **tokens**: what each model call sent and received, what came from the cache, and the total per run. Budgets are set in tokens too. AWFlow never works out a price, so no screen shows money.

Some models don't report their token use. AWFlow then estimates it and marks the number as an estimate.

<Aside type="note" title="Upgrading from turn traces">
Earlier versions kept a short "turn trace" for each reply under **Settings › Privacy**. Those old traces are deleted when you update. The settings carry over: **Keep traces** becomes **Record traces**, and **Developer mode** becomes **Open traces in developer view**. Prompts and replies are now kept by default. A one-time notice tells you, and you can turn **Include prompts and replies** off here.
</Aside>

## Good to know

- Pinned traces are only deleted by **Clear traces**, never by the automatic clean-up.
- Settings apply to this browser. Another browser or device keeps its own traces and settings.
- Experiments and Insights reports record their own traces. They never count in Monitor, but their tokens count toward the agent's budget.
