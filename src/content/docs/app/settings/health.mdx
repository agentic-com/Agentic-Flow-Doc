**In the app:** **Settings › Health**

**Health** answers one question: *is my setup safe?* Every check runs when you open the page, on this device, in a few seconds. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## What it checks

| Check | What it looks for | The fix it offers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Connections without a passphrase | Connections kept on this device that aren't protected by the [secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/) | **Set up a passphrase** |
| Keys pasted into steps | An API key typed in plain text into a step's settings | **Move to a connection** (see below), or **Open the step** |
| Browser may delete data | The browser hasn't promised to keep AWFlow's data when space runs low | **Protect my data** |
| No recent backup | No [backup](/app/settings/storage/#backups) in the last 30 days | **Back up now** |
| Workflows failing on their own | A scheduled workflow that failed its last 5 runs | **Open the last failed run**, or **Turn off** |
| Storage almost full | AWFlow uses 85 % or more of the space your browser allows | **Open Storage** |
| Withdrawn marketplace workflows | A workflow you installed was withdrawn from the marketplace; it no longer runs | **Open the workflow** |
| Active workflows with disabled steps | A workflow that is On has [disabled steps](/app/workflows/components/nodes/#disable-a-step), which background runs skip | **Open the workflow** |
| Fields that refer to a missing step | A field reading `$('Name')` of a step that no longer exists | **Open the step** |
| Very large workflows | A workflow with so many steps it may open slowly | **Open the workflow** |
| Site access nothing uses | AWFlow can read pages on a site no workflow needs any more (extension only) | **Remove access** |

### Move a key to a connection

**Move to a connection** (in Health, and in the canvas's Workflow doctor) does the move in one sheet, **Move the key to a connection**:

- **Found**: where the key was (a header, a Bearer token, or a parameter in the address), masked: the key is never shown in full.
- **Connection type**, guessed from where it sat, and the connection's **name**.

**Move** creates the connection (stored like your other connections, in the vault on this device, and limited to the step's site), points the step at it and removes the field from the step, with **Undo**. If the workflow was published to the Marketplace with the key inside, an amber note says so (*was shared before*): create a new key at the service and paste it into the connection.

## How results are shown

Findings are grouped by importance: **Critical**, **Worth a look** and **For your information**, each with a plain sentence and links to the items concerned. Checks that passed fold into one row at the end. A check that takes too long says **Couldn't check** instead of guessing.

Fixes that change something (**Turn off**, **Remove access**) ask first and name exactly what they will do. After a fix, the check runs again.

## Where you see it

When something critical is found, a dot appears on **Settings** and Home shows a **Critical · Health** chip with the count. Results are kept for 10 minutes; **Run checks again** checks at once.
