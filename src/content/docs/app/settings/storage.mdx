## How much space is used

A bar shows the space used out of what your browser allows, split into:

- **Local models**
- **Memory** (with how much it grew this week)
- **Knowledge bases**
- **Workflows & run history**
- **Other**: app cache, settings and browser overhead

Click **Manage** next to a category to open its page.

## Keep your data safe from clean-up

When the disk is low, a browser may delete website and extension data on its own. Click **Keep my data** to ask the browser to protect AWFlow's storage. The card then reads *Protected from automatic clean-up*.

The browser decides whether to agree. It often does once you use AWFlow more, or after you bookmark it.

<Aside type="caution">
Even protected data is removed if you clear the browser's site data or uninstall the extension. Export what matters first, for example with **Export memory** or [workflow export](/app/workflows/export-import/).
</Aside>

## Clean-up

- **Tell me when memory gets large** shows a suggestion when memory passes 500 MB or the browser is nearly full. On by default.
- **Clean up old conversations** is off by default. When you turn it on, it acts on conversations you haven't opened for a number of days (90 by default): it can *slim* them, *archive* them (export, then remove) or *delete* them. Choose which kinds of chats it covers and whether to skip pinned ones. A preview tells you what it would do right now. It runs once a day; **Run now** runs it immediately and **History** shows past runs.

See [Memory › Storage & clean-up](/app/memory/storage/) for more.
