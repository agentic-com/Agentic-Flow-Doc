## How much space is used

A bar shows the space used out of what your browser allows, split into:

- **Local models**
- **Memory** (with how much it grew this week)
- **Knowledge bases**
- **Workflows & run history**, including the step data kept with runs (see [Workflow settings](/app/workflows/settings/#run-history)). **Step data by workflow** lists it per workflow, biggest first, each with **Change** (its run history setting; *set by the author* for an installed workflow)
- **Other**: app cache, settings and browser overhead

Click **Manage** next to a category to open its page.

## Keep your data safe from clean-up

When the disk is low, a browser may delete website and extension data on its own. Click **Keep my data** to ask the browser to protect AWFlow's storage. The card then reads *Protected from automatic clean-up*.

The browser decides whether to agree. It often does once you use AWFlow more, or after you bookmark it.

<Aside type="caution">
Even protected data is removed if you clear the browser's site data or uninstall the extension. Export what matters first, for example with **Export memory** or [workflow export](/app/workflows/export-import/).
</Aside>


## Backups

A **backup** is one encrypted file (`.awbk`) with everything kept on this device, that you control. Use it to move to another computer, or to recover if the browser loses its data.

### Back up now

1. In **Settings › Storage › Backups**, press **Back up now**.
2. Pick the passphrase that protects the file:
   - **Use my vault passphrase** (offered first when you have a [secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/)): the one that protects your connections, nothing new to remember. If the vault is unlocked, there is nothing to type (*Vault unlocked: ready*); if it is locked, type the vault passphrase once: it unlocks the vault for this session too.
   - **Choose a different one**: type a passphrase twice.

   AWFlow never stores a passphrase: without it, the file can't be opened. Anyone with the file and the passphrase can open it, and changing your vault passphrase later doesn't change old backups.
3. Press **Create backup** (or **Unlock and back up**). The file downloads.

With the vault passphrase, the vault keeps only a key made from it, which can't be read back, for as long as the vault is unlocked; each backup makes its own key from it. The file never holds your vault key.

The backup holds:

- **Workflows**, with their active version, tags and folders;
- **Connections**, re-encrypted with your passphrase;
- your settings and **Data Store collections**.

Not included: memory and knowledge bases. They have their own export (see [Memory](/app/memory/export-import/) and [Knowledge bases](/app/knowledge-bases/export-import-backup/)).

**Last backup** shows when you last made one, and whether it worked.

### Backup schedule

- **Off**.
- **Remind me weekly**: AWFlow reminds you once a week ("Time for your weekly backup"); one click backs up.
- **Back up weekly to a folder**: pick a folder once; AWFlow writes a backup there every week and keeps the last 4 files. Only in browsers that let a page write to a folder (Chrome, Edge). With **Same as your vault** as the passphrase, the weekly backup runs by itself while the vault is unlocked (*· ran by itself*); while it is locked, the card and the reminder say *Backup due · your vault is locked*, with **Unlock and back up**. With **Your own, typed each time**, your passphrase is asked when a backup is ready to finish.

When a backup is due, the reminder shows in the app. In the extension it also reaches you when AWFlow is closed: the first time you use the browser after it is due, a system notification says *Time for your weekly backup*, with **Back up now** (it opens Storage with the backup sheet) and **Tomorrow** (it waits a day). One per week. The Backups card has **Send a test notification**, and says when your computer blocks the browser's notifications (*How to allow them*); then reminders show only while AWFlow is open. The web app shows the reminder only while it is open.

### Restore from a file

1. Press **Restore from a file…** and pick the `.awbk` file.
2. Type its passphrase. A wrong passphrase imports nothing. A backup made with the vault passphrase says so (*This backup used your vault passphrase of 12 Oct*).
3. Check the counts, then choose:
   - **Add as copies**: nothing here is overwritten.
   - **Replace items with the same id**: items already here are replaced.
4. Press **Restore**.

Restored workflows are **Off** until you switch them on. Every workflow is checked like an imported `.awf` file.

### Protect your data after the first save

The first time you save a workflow on this device, if the browser hasn't promised to keep AWFlow's data, a card offers **Keep your work safe on this device**: **Protect my data** asks the browser (the same as **Keep my data** above). **Not now** hides it for 30 days. If the browser says no, the card offers **Set up backups**.

## Clean-up

- **Tell me when memory gets large** shows a suggestion when memory passes 500 MB or the browser is nearly full. On by default.
- **Clean up old conversations** is off by default. When you turn it on, it acts on conversations you haven't opened for a number of days (90 by default): it can *slim* them, *archive* them (export, then remove) or *delete* them. Choose which kinds of chats it covers and whether to skip pinned ones. A preview tells you what it would do right now. It runs once a day; **Run now** runs it immediately and **History** shows past runs.

See [Memory › Storage & clean-up](/app/memory/storage/) for more.
