## Language

Pick English, French, German or Chinese. The app switches straight away.

## Appearance

Under **Appearance Settings**, choose **Light** or **Dark** and click **Update preferences**.

## Approval sound

**Play a sound when your approval is needed** plays a short chime when a run pauses for you. When the app isn't in front of you, the desktop notification carries the sound instead. It is on by default. See [Notifications](/app/notifications/#runs-waiting-for-you).

## Aria's look

Choose the mascot that draws Aria everywhere in the app: **Ember Mochi**, a squishy little spirit with a glowing flame and the official AWFlow mascot, or **Pip the Owlet**, a round, curious owl who pulls its glasses down to work. Each card shows a live preview. She works the same either way. You can also pick it on her Identity tab: see [Aria's look](/app/chat-and-agents/agent-builder/#arias-look).

## Agent animations

How much your agents move:

- **Lively**: faces move and react, and eyes follow your pointer.
- **Calm**: faces move and react.
- **Off**: still faces.

Status marks, like the badge and ring that show an agent needs your approval, always show, whatever you pick. If your system asks to reduce motion, faces stay still. What each face means is in [What the face shows](/app/chat-and-agents/agent-builder/#what-the-face-shows).

## Default storage location

Signed-in users choose where new workflows and credentials are saved: **In your account (cloud)** or **On this device**. Cloud is the default. You can still choose each time you create something. This option is hidden in a local workspace, where everything is local.

## Local secret protection

Protect the credentials stored in this browser with a passphrase. See [Local secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/).

## Data store

Lists the values your workflows remember between runs with the [Data Store](/app/data-store/) node, grouped by workflow. Delete one value, or **Clear all** for a group. These values live in this browser only.
