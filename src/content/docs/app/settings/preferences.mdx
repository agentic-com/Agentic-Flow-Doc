**Settings › Preferences** controls how the app looks and behaves on this device.

## Language

Pick English, French, German or Chinese. The app switches straight away.

## Appearance

Choose **Light** or **Dark** and click **Update preferences**.

## Approval sound

**Play a sound when your approval is needed** plays a short chime when a run pauses for you. When the app isn't in front of you, the desktop notification carries the sound instead. It is on by default. See [Notifications](/app/notifications/#runs-waiting-for-you).

## Agent animations

How much your agents' faces move:

- **Lively**: blinks, glances, and eyes that follow your pointer.
- **Calm**: blinks and states only.
- **Off**: still faces.

If your system asks to reduce motion, faces stay still whatever you pick.

## Default storage location

Signed-in users choose where new workflows and credentials are saved: **Cloud (synced to your account)** or **Local (this browser only)**. Cloud is the default. You can still choose each time you create something. This option is hidden in a local workspace, where everything is local.

## Local secret protection

Protect the credentials stored in this browser with a passphrase. See [Local secret vault](/app/account/secret-vault/).

## Data store

Lists the values your workflows remember between runs with the [Data Store](/app/data-store/) node, grouped by workflow. Delete one value, or **Clear all** for a group. These values live in this browser only.
