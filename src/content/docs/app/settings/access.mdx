**Settings › Site access** lists the websites (hosts) and connected apps each workflow is allowed to use.

## How access is granted

You don't add entries here. A workflow asks the first time it needs a website or an app's credential, and you choose to allow it. If you tick **Always allow for this workflow**, the permission is saved and shows up on this page. Installed and imported workflows always ask; your own workflows ask only when [Safe mode](/app/settings/security/) is on.

## Review and change access

Each workflow has a card with its hosts and, under **Apps**, its apps. Everything happens on this page:

A workflow you deleted appears as *Removed workflow (no longer exists)*. You can remove its entries.

When nothing has been granted yet, the page says *No site or app access granted yet*.

<Aside type="caution">
These permissions always apply to workflows you installed. For workflows **you created**, they only apply when [Safe mode](/app/settings/security/) is on. A banner reminds you when it's off.
</Aside>

Permissions are saved **on this device only**. [Health](/app/settings/health/) lists sites AWFlow can read that no workflow uses any more, with **Remove access**.

To clear all permissions of an installed workflow at once, you can also use **Reset permissions** in its row menu on the [workflows list](/app/workflows/manage-list/).
