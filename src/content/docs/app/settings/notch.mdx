**Settings › Assistant Notch** controls the floating bubble that the extension pins to the edge of web pages. It gives you quick actions (the same as the right-click menu) and the chat workflows available on the current page. Read [Assistant Notch](/app/assistant-notch/) for how to use it.

## Turn it on and customise it

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar and click **Assistant Notch**. Everything happens on that page: hover or tap a step to find it on the screen.
