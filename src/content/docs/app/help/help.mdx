When you build workflows with AWFlow, you may have questions, run into a problem, or want examples to learn from. This page lists where to get help.

## Help in the app

**Help** (at the bottom of the sidebar, in **More** in the side panel, or from the command palette) opens a Help sheet:

- **Ask Aria about AWFlow**: Aria answers questions about the app in plain words and links the right page of these docs.
- **On this page**: **Replay the tour** where the page has one, and **Docs for this page**.
- **What's new**: the latest three releases. A dot on **Help** means there's something you haven't seen.
- **Feedback**: **Send feedback** (a problem, an idea or a question, up to 2,000 characters; you can add the app version and the page you're on, never your workflows or secrets; your email if you aren't signed in), and **Request a step or integration** on the [request board](/app/request-board/).
- **Learn & community** links, and GitHub links for developers.

Pages that used to start a tour now show a **?** next to their title: it pulses once on your first visit and offers **Show me around**.

Under a failed run's explanation, **Did this explain the problem?** asks for a thumbs up or down; 👎 lets you tell more.

The community links below are also in the Help sheet.

## Where to get help

### Community forum
The <a href="https://community.awflow.io/" target="_blank">**Community forum**</a> is the main place to ask questions, share workflows, and get help from other users and the team. It's searchable, so the answer to your question may already be there.

### Report a bug
If something doesn't work as these docs describe, <a href="https://github.com/awflow/awflow-Studio-Issues/issues/new?template=%F0%9F%90%9E-bug-report.md" target="_blank">open a bug report on GitHub</a>. You need a free GitHub account.

### Request a feature
To suggest an improvement to the app, <a href="https://github.com/awflow/awflow-Studio-Issues/issues/new?template=%F0%9F%92%A1-feature-request.md" target="_blank">open a feature request on GitHub</a>. You can see what's planned on the <a href="https://github.com/orgs/awflow/projects/4" target="_blank">public roadmap</a>.

Looking for a ready-made workflow rather than a new feature? Ask for it on the [request board](/app/request-board/).

### Email
For account questions, or anything you'd rather not post in public, email <a href="mailto:hello@awflow.io">hello@awflow.io</a>.

## What to include when asking for help

The more detail you give, the faster others can help. Include:

- **The steps you took** before the problem happened, so others can reproduce it.
- **Screenshots or short recordings** of what you saw.
- **The documentation pages** you followed.
- **An export of the workflow** (see [Export and import](/app/workflows/export-import/)). Remove any personal data first.
- **Error messages** shown in the app.

---

## Tips for good community posts

- Use a clear title and describe what you tried.
- Share the smallest workflow that shows the problem.
- Search first: the answer may already exist.
- Reply to follow-up questions so others can help.

Good posts help you and everyone who runs into the same problem later.
