A **team** is a group of agents with a lead, a way of working and shared rules. Every conversation with a team is a **team thread**: you give the team a job, the agents hand work to each other, and the lead comes back with one answer.

Open **Agents › Teams**.

## Create a team

- **Describe a team** ("A crew that researches a topic, writes a blog post and turns it into 3 social posts") and click **Suggest a team** — Aria picks members from your agents and suggests new ones.
- Or start from a **team template** (Research desk, Inbox zero, Job hunt, Shop smart, Trip crew, Content studio), or **New team**.

## Ways of working

| Mode | How it works | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lead routes** | The lead reads your message, hands parts to the right members, then merges their results into one answer. | Varied requests |
| **Pipeline** | Members work in a fixed order; each gets your request plus the previous member's result. The lead can check the final result. | Repeatable jobs |
| **Open table** | You — or any member — @mention whoever should answer. | Brainstorming |

**Who can hand work to whom** is a map: click one agent, then another, to add or remove a connection. A handoff carries **the task and the results it needs**, plus a short slice of the thread so far — the thread's summary and its last few turns, fitted to the member's model (at most about a third of its context window). Members know what was already said without reading your whole chat, and small models stay within their context.

Thread options: show agent-to-agent messages, let agents work in parallel, @mention any member, have the lead check results before replying, and allow guest agents.

## Rules and limits

**Team rules** sit on top of each agent's own rules and can **only make them stricter**: for submitting forms, opening new sites, using your own tabs and running workflows choose *Agent's rule*, *Always ask* or *Never*. Payments, passwords, sending messages as you and deleting data always ask, whatever the rules say.

**Approvals** appear as a card in the team thread and as a desktop notification. If nobody answers within the wait time (30 minutes by default), the step is skipped.

**Limits per thread** stop runaway loops and keep cloud usage predictable:

- Max steps for all agents together (default 60)
- Max handoffs between agents (12)
- Agents working at once (2)

When a limit is reached, the thread **pauses and asks you** or **stops**. A paused thread shows a **Not done yet** card with **Continue · 30 more steps** and **Change the limit**.

A team can also have a monthly **token budget**, set on its **Rules & limits** tab. It warns you at 80% and, at 100%, notifies you, switches to on-device models or pauses the team. It replaces the old cloud-token cap per thread. See [Alerts & budgets](/app/observability/alerts-and-budgets/).

**Shared by the team**: team memory (facts every member can read, plus the memories an agent shared with **this** team in its Memory tab — see [Team memory](/app/chat-and-agents/memory/#team-memory)), team [knowledge bases](/app/chat-and-agents/knowledge/#teams-and-projects) (each member also uses its own), and one browser tab group that members share.

## Team threads

Open a team to see its **Threads**. Type a new job to start one, or start a thread from anywhere:

- In any chat: `@trip-crew 3 days in Rome in March`
- Address bar: `aw @trip-crew …`
- Right-click a page: **Send to a team…**
- From a team routine: each run is a new thread.

In a thread you see which agent answered, the handoffs as pills (click one to read that member's result), each member's tool steps as they happen, approval cards, and a side panel with each member's status, the plan, the team rules and usage against the limits. Under **Cloud tokens**, the panel lists each member's usage (tokens in, out and cached), most expensive first.

The members' faces at the top of the thread all look at whoever is speaking: the member a task was handed to, or else the one that is working. Each face also shows what that member is doing; see [What the face shows](/app/chat-and-agents/agent-builder/#what-the-face-shows).

A handoff pill reads **Failed** when the member hit an error (expand it to see why), and **Skipped** when it was stopped, ran into a limit, or an approval was declined or not answered. Members tell the lead whether they finished, only partly finished, were blocked or need you, so the lead can decide what to do next. Threads keep working when you switch to another page of the app while the browser is open. If you close the app before the team has finished, the thread shows **This request was interrupted** the next time you open it — click **Retry** to send the same request again.

**Invite guest agent** adds an agent to this thread only — the team doesn't change.

## Manage a team

The team page has tabs for **Threads**, **Members** (roles, who each can hand work to, model, status; add, remove, make lead), **How they work**, **Rules & limits**, **Shared memory** and **Routines**.

**Shared memory** lists the team's own facts and, under **Shared by members**, the facts each agent shared with this team — **Stop sharing** keeps one with its agent only. Deleting a team asks whether to delete its memories too. See [Team memory](/app/chat-and-agents/memory/#team-memory).

A thread starts with the team's setup at that moment. When you change the team, new threads use the new setup, and AWFlow asks whether running threads should **keep it as is** or **apply** the change.

## Save a chat as a team

When two or more agents have joined a normal chat, Aria offers **Save … as a team?** — one click opens the wizard with those members.

## Observe a team

Every team thread is recorded as traces in **Observability**: pick **Teams** as the source on the **Traces** tab to see each agent's turns and handoffs, and use **Monitor** to follow the team's runs and tokens over time. See [Traces](/app/observability/traces/) and [Monitor](/app/observability/monitor/).

## Share a team

**Share as template** publishes the team to the marketplace or downloads it. See [Agent & team templates](/app/chat-and-agents/templates/).
