**In the app:** sidebar › **Agents**

An **agent** is an assistant with a job: its own instructions, model, skills, knowledge and rules. Aria is your built-in agent; you can edit it, but not delete it. A *persona* is simply an agent without skills. Agents live in your browser, use your logins when they browse, and are stored **only on this device**.

## Create an agent

Open **Agents** in the sidebar:

- **Describe an agent**: one sentence ("Every Monday, check 3 competitor pricing pages and post changes to Slack"). Aria drafts the agent for you to adjust.
- **Start from a template**: Chief of staff, Inbox triager, Price watcher, Study buddy…
- **Blank agent**, or **Import** an agent or team file.
- Browse community agents and teams in [Marketplace › Agents & teams](/app/chat-and-agents/templates/).

Click **Chat** on a card to talk to that agent. Open it to edit it in the [builder](/app/chat-and-agents/agent-builder/); changes save automatically.

## Use it everywhere

| Where | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Pick the agent in the chat, or type its `@handle`. It also answers in [projects](/app/chat-and-agents/projects/) and [team threads](/app/chat-and-agents/teams/). |
| **Another agent** | With the **Teammates** skill, an agent can hand part of a task to it. |
| **A workflow** | The [Run Agent](/nodes/builtin/ai/aiagents/runagentnode/) node runs it as a step. A [routine](#routines) is exactly that: a trigger followed by Run Agent. |

<Callout type="info" title="Your data" href="/app/privacy-and-data/" linkLabel="Privacy & your data">

Agents, their chats and their memories are stored **only in this browser**. When an agent uses a cloud model, its messages go from your browser to that provider with your key; with an on-device model, nothing leaves your device. Actions are real: emails send and sheets change, so its [rules](/app/chat-and-agents/browser-control/) decide what it may do alone.

</Callout>

## Routines

A routine is an ordinary [workflow](/app/workflows/create/): a trigger followed by a **Run agent** node. Create one from the agent's **Routines** tab:

- **On a schedule**: daily, weekly or a custom time.
- **When a page opens**: match a site or address.
- **When a page changes**: watch a price or an element.
- **Show it once**: do it yourself while the agent watches; your steps are recorded as a workflow.
- Or run one of your existing workflows.

Routines run while your browser is open. In the background, actions the agent's rules mark *ask* are skipped and listed in **Activity** for you to review. Each run's answer is saved as a chat. Open the routine's workflow in the canvas to change it.

## Activity

**Activity** in the sidebar lists what your agents did: answers, routine runs, approvals waiting for you and skipped actions, grouped by day. Click an entry to open the chat or thread.

## Share an agent

**Share** in the builder publishes the agent to the marketplace or downloads it as a file. See [Agent & team templates](/app/chat-and-agents/templates/) for what is, and isn't, included.
