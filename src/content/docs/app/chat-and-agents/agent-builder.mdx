## The builder tabs

The tabs sit in the header, next to the agent's name. In a narrow window they move to a row under the name.

| Tab | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| **Identity** | Its name, @handle and role at the top, then its look (in the look studio), greeting and conversation starters. **Try it · chat · live states** shows it in an example chat, a team thread and the agent list. |
| **Instructions** | How it works and what it avoids. Use variables like `{today}`, `{my name}`, `{current tab}` and `{memory}`. **Improve** and **Generate** ask your model for help; earlier versions are kept. |
| **Model** | *Same as Aria* or a specific on-device, Ollama (detected automatically when it is running) or cloud model, its creativity, max steps per answer, and whether to fall back to on-device when you're offline. |
| **Skills** | What it can do: **Browser control**, **Web search**, **Workflows**, **On-device AI**, **Teammates**, **MCP** servers. See [Give it skills](/app/chat-and-agents/agent-skills/). |
| **Knowledge** | The knowledge bases it uses in every chat, routine and team it joins, when to look things up, whether it shows sources or may search on its own, and **Try it**. See [Knowledge for an agent](/app/chat-and-agents/knowledge/#knowledge-for-an-agent). |
| **Memory** | Whether it learns from chats, whether it also reads Aria's memory, its suggested facts, and its memories: edit, forget, or share one with a team. See [Assistant memory](/app/chat-and-agents/memory/#the-agents-memory-tab). |
| **Rules** | What it does alone, what it asks about, and what it never does. See [Browser control & safety](/app/chat-and-agents/browser-control/). |
| **Routines** | Run it on a schedule, when a page opens or changes, or record the steps once (*Show it once*). See [Routines](/app/chat-and-agents/agents/#routines). |

A cloud model needs a key in **Settings › Providers**: see [All connections](/app/connections/) for your provider.

## Try it

The right panel lets you **Try it**: chat with the agent as you edit, and on the Rules tab, **Test a rule** with a sentence like "click Reserve on booking.example".

**Try it** runs the **real agent**: its model, skills, rules, approvals and max steps, exactly what a chat with it would use. A row of chips shows what is in effect (the model and where it runs, each skill, marked *off* when it can't be used, the rules preset and the step limit). Edits apply on your next message.

:::caution[Actions are real]
Emails send and sheets change. Approvals appear in the panel, the same as in a chat.
:::

You see the same [work timeline, step limit card and approval receipts](/app/chat-and-agents/seeing-the-work/) as in a chat, and a banner when a skill is off. **Reset** starts over with a fresh chat, tools and browser session.

## Looks and living eyes

Every agent is a character. Its face shows what it is doing, in chats, in the agent list and in team threads, so you can tell at a glance which agent is busy and which one needs you.

### What the face shows

| What you see | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Blinks and glances around | **Idle**: nothing to do right now. |
| Eyes drift up, a thought bubble | **Thinking**: it is working out an answer. |
| Eyes scan side to side, a spinner | **Working**: browsing a page or using a tool. |
| Mouth moves with the words | **Speaking**: its reply is coming in. |
| Looks at you and bounces, an orange "?" | **Needs you**: it is waiting for your ok. |
| Happy squint and a hop, sparkles | **Done**: it just finished. |
| Worried brows, a sweat drop | **Can't**: a step failed or a skill isn't available. |
| Eyes closed, "zzz" | **Offline**: its model isn't ready yet. |

When an agent is waiting for an approval you can answer, its picture in **Agents & teams** also gets a pulsing orange **needs-you ring** until you answer.

At the top of a team thread, the members' faces sit side by side and all **look at whoever is speaking**, so you can follow the conversation. See [Teams](/app/chat-and-agents/teams/).

A new agent's look comes from its name and role, and changes as you type them, until you change any option yourself. From then on, renaming keeps your look; click **Generate one from the name** under the role to start again from the name. Its @handle also follows the name until its first chat, so @mentions you already use never break.

Agents made before the new faces show an **Upgrade face** switch above the pickers. Turn it on to add brows, a mouth and cheeks; shape, colour and wardrobe stay. Turn it off to go back.

:::note[Describe a look needs a model]
**Ask Aria** uses the model you set up for Aria on the **Assistant** page (on-device, Ollama or a cloud provider). Without one, the studio says so and nothing changes. The other controls work without a model.
:::

New agents get a look that suits their job; press **Try another look** before creating one. Aria's look (orange, with sun rays) can't be changed, so you always recognize her; her face still shows what she is doing.

### Calm the motion

Go to **Settings › Preferences › Agent animations**: **Lively** (faces move and react, and eyes follow your pointer), **Calm** (faces move and react) or **Off** (still faces). Status marks, like the needs-you badge and ring, always show, whatever you pick. When your system asks to reduce motion, faces stay still. See [Preferences](/app/settings/preferences/#agent-animations).
