Aria can build a workflow for you and keep refining it for the whole conversation. Switch the chat to **Build**, say what to automate, then keep asking for changes ("also post it to Slack"). Every change is shown in the chat and can be undone.

## Chat or Build

The composer has a **Chat | Build** switch. It is per conversation and stays where you leave it.

| Mode | What your messages do |
| --- | --- |
| **Chat** | Aria answers. Nothing is built. |
| **Build** | Every message changes the workflow this chat is about. |

Press **⌥↵** (Alt+Enter) instead of Enter to send one message the other way: **just ask** in Build, **build this once** in Chat. The mode doesn't change. The composer shows the hint under the box, and in Build it gets a highlighted ring.

You can also type **/build** at the start of a message to build once while in Chat.

In Build, obvious questions ("Why did you add a filter?") are answered without touching the workflow. The reply says **Looked like a question · no changes**, with **Apply as a change** if you did mean it as an edit. Messages that start like an edit ("Also add a delay") are built.

## Which workflow a chat builds

In Build, the chat is **bound to one workflow**. The first time you turn Build on:

- With nothing in the chat yet, Aria builds a **new workflow** when you send your first message.
- Otherwise, **Build which workflow?** opens:

| Choice | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **In this chat** | Continue a workflow already in this conversation. |
| **New workflow** | Start a fresh one. |
| **Your workflows** | Search your saved workflows (Active or Inactive) and improve one. |

The header shows the workflow's name as a pill. Click it to open **Build which workflow?** and pick another workflow (your other drafts in this chat are kept), **New workflow**, or **Stop building** to switch back to Chat.

The home cards help you start: **Automate something** turns on Build for this chat, and **Improve one of my workflows** opens the picker on *Your workflows*.

## A plan first, for bigger builds

When you ask for something bigger (more than 3 actions, a branch, or more than 2 apps), Aria first shows a **plan card** before building anything: a summary, the trigger, numbered steps with app icons, and up to 5 questions to answer (one choice, several, or free text).

- **Build** builds the approved plan with your answers.
- **Change** asks what should change and re-plans once, marking the changed steps.
- **Cancel** ends the turn with no draft.

Turn on **Plan first** in the message box to always get a plan (remembered per place: the Aria page, the side panel, the canvas dock). The notch keeps building straight away.

## While it builds

Aria builds with the **whole conversation so far**, so details you gave a few messages earlier ("only invoices over €500") are taken into account.

A **Building your workflow** card shows each stage as it runs, with its time: *Understand your request*, *Look for similar workflows*, *Choose an approach*, *Write the workflow* and *Check it's valid* (plus *Fixing the problems* when the check finds something). When it is done, the card folds into a line such as **Built in 0:14 · 5 stages**; click it to see the stages again.

The **preview draws the workflow as it is written**. AI building blocks (a chat model, memory, tools, an output parser) are attached to their agent's slots, not chained as steps. Steps after an **If** or **Route by value** are laid out in side-by-side lanes, one per branch, in the order of the node's outputs, and each branch is wired to the right output.

**Stop** on the card really stops the build: the model call in progress is cancelled and no more requests go to the model, so a stopped build costs nothing further. A **Build stopped** receipt stays in the chat with the stages that ran, *Nothing was changed*, **Build again** and **Edit request**.

### Settings to check

When the new workflow needs something from you before it can run — a credential, a sheet ID, a channel — a card lists **N settings to check before it runs**, one row per step. Click a row to open the preview on that step. The card also says when connections were **fixed automatically**.

## Changes, undo and versions

Each build reply ends with a change chip, such as **Invoice saver · v3 · +1 step**. It counts steps added, changed and removed since the previous version.

- **View changes** opens the preview with the differences highlighted.
- **Undo** reverts the **latest** change only, and goes back one version. Older changes can't be undone from the chip.
- **Keep building &lt;name&gt;** appears after a one-off build made in Chat: it turns Build on for that workflow.
- **Review & save** appears when the workflow can be saved (see below).

If a message produces nothing usable, no version is added.

## Improving an existing workflow

Pick a saved workflow in the picker, or choose **Improve with Aria** in a workflow's row menu in the Workflows list. Aria opens the most recent chat about that workflow, or starts a new one with Build on.

Changes to a saved workflow are **staged in the chat**; the workflow itself is untouched. A banner reminds you: **N unsaved changes — &lt;name&gt; still runs the saved version.** with **Discard** and **Review & save**.

**Review & save** lists what changed and asks **Save changes to &lt;name&gt;?**:

- **Save into &lt;name&gt;** updates the saved workflow.
- **Save as a new workflow** keeps the original as it is and saves the result as a copy; the chat then builds the copy.

**Discard** throws away the staged changes and goes back to the saved version.

When Aria changes an existing workflow, it asks the model for a short list of edits (add, change, rename, remove, connect, disconnect) and applies them itself: settings it doesn't touch, connections, names, positions and notes stay exactly as they were, so the list of changes is exact. A change plan also counts **links** that changed, so a draft that only rewires steps can be kept. Aria checks its draft with the **workflow doctor** (a loop without an exit, a branch going nowhere, a secret typed into a field, a missing connection…), fixes what it can and lists the rest as settings to check.

A workflow built from scratch is saved with the usual save dialog. After that, the chat remembers it, so saving again updates it instead of creating another one.

## Fix with Aria

When a step or a run fails, **Fix with Aria** (on the failure card, in the step's **Output**, and in a failed run's menu in **Runs**) opens Aria on the failure. Before you send anything, it shows exactly what it will share: the error, the step with its settings (connections replaced by their name), the shape of the input without its values, and the steps before it. A **sample of the data** is only included if you switch it on (on by default only with an on-device model, at most 2,000 characters). Aria explains the problem and proposes a change: **Keep** applies it as one undo step. If the workflow changed since that run, Aria says so.

- A connection error offers **Help me connect** instead.
- Code steps offer **Write this code with Aria**: it reads the last input's shape and your current code, and answers with a change you **Keep** or **Undo**.

## Mentioning another workflow

Type **@** and pick a workflow to bring it into one message. A chip appears above the box, **&lt;name&gt; · reference**, where you choose its role:

| Role | What Aria does | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use as reference** | Reads it and borrows from it. It isn't changed. | "Build a weekly report like @invoice-saver" |
| **Change it** | Applies one change to that workflow, staged as above. Your current workflow stays the target. | "@invoice-saver also save a copy to Drive" |
| **New workflow from it** | Copies it into a new workflow (named "&lt;name&gt; (copy)"), turns Build on for it and applies the rest of your message. | "Make a version of @invoice-saver for receipts" |
| **Ask about it** | Answers questions about it. No changes. | "What does @invoice-saver do with failed rows?" |

Aria picks a role from your wording and the mode, and a hint under the box says what will happen. Your choice in the chip always wins. Mention one workflow per message.

You can still @-mention [agents and teams](/app/chat-and-agents/agents/) in Chat as before.

## Where it works

- **Aria page** and the **[side panel](/app/side-panel/)** — full support, including the workflow pill, banner and Review & save.
- **Canvas dock** and the **[notch](/app/assistant-notch/)** — the same **Chat | Build** switch and **⌥↵**. Canvas dock chats belong to their workflow: see [Canvas dock chats](#canvas-dock-chats).

:::note
Aria builds with whichever [model you picked](/app/chat-and-agents/chat/). Small on-device models can miss details, so check **View changes** before you save.
:::

## Canvas dock chats

The Aria dock in the canvas saves its chats **with the workflow** you have open:

- Close the canvas and come back later: the last chat **reopens** where you left it.
- The **Saved chats** button (a clock with a count) in the dock header lists this workflow's chats. Click one to switch to it, or use the bin to delete it. **New chat** starts another one. **All in Memory →** opens the [Memory page](/app/memory/overview/).
- Dock chats are listed on the Memory page under **Canvas dock**, not in Aria's chat list.
- When you delete the workflow, its dock chats are offered for deletion with it.

A change the dock was proposing but you hadn't kept yet is not saved with the chat: keep or discard it before you leave.
