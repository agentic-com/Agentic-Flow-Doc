The browser updates the AWFlow extension by itself, usually within a day of a release. AWFlow 0.9 tells you, before and after, whether an update touches your workflows, and keeps your data safe while it upgrades.

## The update card on Home

When an update is waiting, or has just been installed, and one of **your own** workflows uses a step that changes in it, a card appears on **Home**:

- **AWFlow 0.9 is ready**: the new version is downloaded and waits for the browser to apply it. The card says how many of your workflows use a step that changes.
- **Updated to AWFlow 0.9**: the update is installed, and some of your workflows use a step that changed.

From the card:

- **Review** lists the workflows and, for each, the steps concerned. **Open** opens a workflow with those steps ringed on the canvas. Nothing is modified.
- **Update now** applies the waiting version at once (the app reloads).
- **Release notes** opens the [release notes](/releases/) of that version.
- **Later** hides the card until the next version.

Updating never changes your saved workflows: steps keep their [version](/app/workflows/step-versions/) and workflows their [behaviour](/app/workflows/behaviour-versions/) until you choose to update them.

## "AWFlow needs an update"

If AWFlow is too old to talk to your account, a banner says **AWFlow needs an update to keep syncing. Your local workflows keep working.** Cloud saves and syncing stop until you update; everything kept on this device works as usual. Press **Update AWFlow**, or update the extension from your browser's extensions page.

## Your saved data after an update

An update can change how AWFlow stores your data on this device. Before it changes anything, AWFlow keeps a backup of what it is about to change. If the change can't be finished:

- **We couldn't update your saved data. Nothing was lost.** Saving is paused until the update works; you can still open and read everything. Use **Try again**, or **Export my data** to keep a copy.

If you open your data with an **older** AWFlow than the one that last saved it (for example after rolling back the extension), a banner says **This data was saved by a newer AWFlow. Update AWFlow to edit it.** You can open and read everything, but changes and run history aren't saved until you update.

## When a step is switched off remotely

Rarely, a step can be switched off for everyone while a serious problem is fixed. A workflow that reaches it stops at that step with a plain message, before it does anything. Your workflow is not changed: the step runs again as soon as it is switched back on.

## What AWFlow sends with each request

Every call from the app to the AWFlow service carries a short header with the AWFlow version, whether you use the extension or the web app, and the browser name, for example `0.9.0/ext/chrome`. It lets the service warn old versions and keep them working. It contains nothing about you or your workflows. See [Privacy and your data](/app/privacy-and-data/).
