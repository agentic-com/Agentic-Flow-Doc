**In the app:** the recap on **Home**, and its link to the **Insights** page (`#/app/insights`)

**Insights** show what your workflows do for you over time. They are computed from your runs, on this device for workflows kept here and in your account for cloud workflows.

## The four numbers

Pick a period: **7 days**, **30 days**, **90 days** or **1 year**.

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Runs** | How many runs finished, compared with the period before. |
| **Failure rate** | The share of runs that failed. Runs you stopped don't count. |
| **Typical run time** | About how long a run takes. |
| **Time saved** | Runs multiplied by the minutes each run saves you (see below). |

Below the tiles: the workflow most worth a look (for example the one that failed most), and your top workflows by runs, with their own runs, failure rate, typical time and last failure. Home shows the top 3; the Insights page the top 10 (a table on a wide screen, cards in the side panel).

## Set the time a workflow saves

Time saved needs one number per workflow: how many minutes a run saves you.

1. In the workflow list of Insights, press **Set** next to a workflow (or open [Workflow settings](/app/workflows/settings/#value) › **Value**).
2. Type the **Minutes saved per run**. About 1 minute per step is a good start.
3. **Save**. It is saved as a new version of the workflow.

The value applies to runs from now on. Earlier days keep their numbers.

## Good to know

- Numbers are kept per day when each run finishes, so deleting old runs from Runs never changes past insights.
- When cloud numbers can't load, the page shows this device's numbers and says so.
- With no runs in the period, the page says so and links to your workflows.
