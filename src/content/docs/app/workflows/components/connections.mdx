**Links** are the lines between steps. They say what runs next, and which data goes along. (Before AWFlow 0.9 they were called *connections*; that word now means the accounts you [connect](/app/connections/) to apps.)

Drop on an empty spot instead, and the step picker opens: the step you pick is added there, already linked. Clicking the **+** on a step's output does the same.

## Remove a link or insert a step

Hover a link (or focus it with the keyboard): two buttons appear in its middle. **Delete link** (the bin) removes it; **Add a step here** (the **+**) inserts a new step between the two, and moves the steps after it to the right when there isn't room.

## Steps with several outputs

[If](/nodes/builtin/flow/if/), [Route by value](/nodes/builtin/flow/switch/) and other branching steps have one output per branch, each with a **name** (the branch or rule). A link attaches to that named output:

- **Reordering** branches or rules keeps every link pointing where it was.
- **Renaming** an output removes the link that left it, and the canvas tells you, rather than leaving it pointing at a branch that no longer exists.

Each link carries only the items of its own output: the step on If's **false** branch gets the items that failed the condition, never the others.

A step whose **On error** is **Send to an error output** gets a red **Error** output: link it to handle failed items (see [Error handling](/concepts/flow/error-handling/#send-failed-items-to-an-error-output)).

## Read the links

- After a run, each link shows how many items passed along it.
- Links of a [disabled step](/app/workflows/components/nodes/#disable-a-step) are dashed.
- Screen readers announce a link as "Connection from *Get issues* to *If* (true branch)".

## Tips

- Keep the flow readable, left to right. **Tidy up** (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>) lays it out for you.
- After you link a new step, run it with **Run step** to check its input.
- If a step says **This step has no input**, it isn't linked to anything before it. See [Troubleshooting links](/app/troubleshooting/workflow-connections/).
