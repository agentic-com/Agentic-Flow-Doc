Publishing lets you share your workflow in the Marketplace so other people can use it as a template.

After you publish, others can browse it, import it, and adapt it to their own needs.

---

## Why publish a workflow?

- Showcase your workflow skills and ideas.  
- Help others by sharing a ready-made automation.  
- Encourage reuse of proven solutions rather than reinventing from scratch.  
- Receive feedback, contributions, or improvements from the community.

---

## Before you publish

Make sure your workflow meets these key conditions:

- It’s **saved**, **stable** and **tested** (run it a few times to confirm it works). See [Run from the canvas](/app/workflows/executions/run-from-the-canvas/).  
- Nodes are clearly named and organized.  
- Sticky Notes or documentation are added to explain the workflow logic. (See [Sticky Notes](/app/workflows/components/sticky-notes/))  
- Sensitive credentials or data are removed—replace them with placeholders or variables.  
- If your workflow uses browser nodes, ensure the selectors and paths are generic or documented.  
- Exported workflow JSON works as a standalone template when imported.

---

### Versions and what installers get

Someone who installs your workflow gets it exactly as you published it: each step keeps its [version](/app/workflows/step-versions/), and the workflow keeps its [behaviour](/app/workflows/behaviour-versions/), so it behaves for them as it did for you. A workflow that uses new behaviour or new steps needs a recent AWFlow; older ones are told it was *made with a newer AWFlow*.

When you publish, AWFlow clears what belongs to you: connections, values marked to reset (such as a conversation picked in Chat Memory), **Ask before a call** and trusted sites of AI tools, allowed sites of the Browser block, and your schedule timezone. The [variables](/app/variables/) your workflow reads are listed (names and descriptions, never values) so installers know what to set. Disabled steps are flagged before you publish.

compose a shareable 16:9 image from your workflow — a snapshot of the flow plus
the nodes it uses, its trigger, a few quick stats and an AWFlow mark, arranged

## After publishing

- Your listing goes to a quick moderation review. Once it's approved it's visible in the Marketplace.
- To share a new version of your workflow, use **Release** in the workflows table. Write what's new; the release dialog shows the changes since the last version, any new access it needs, and the fields that stay blank for installers. Fields you chose to blank when you first published stay blank in every later version.
- A release of a live listing is reviewed while the current version stays live. You'll see "v3 sent for review — v2 stays live" and get a notification when it's decided.
- Reply to reviews and keep the description up to date as people use it.
- See how it runs for the people who installed it on its private **Performance** tab, once 10 installs share stats. See [Performance & sharing stats](/app/marketplace/author-stats/).
- You get a notification when a listing is approved ("“X” is live"), sent back with the moderator's note ("“X” needs changes"), or suspended, and when someone leaves a review.
- The review step checks for private keys again every time you tick **Blank for installers**, and marks each fixed value **✓ Fixed**. Values that can't be blanked (for example in the listing text) need to be moved into a credential or removed.
- Step notes you write while editing a live listing are only saved to your workflow once the update is submitted.
- If something blocks publishing, the wizard tells you exactly what: the step and field that look like a private key, or which listing details are still missing, with a button to jump to that step.
- Changing the preview images of a live listing goes through the same quick review as other edits; the current images stay until it's approved. Only images uploaded to AWFlow can be used.

### Stop sharing a listing

In **My published workflows**, **Stop sharing** takes a live listing out of the Marketplace:

- It's hidden from the Marketplace, search and your author page.
- **Everyone who installed it keeps it working.** They see "No longer listed" next to it.
- Reviews, versions and the link are kept. Click **Share again** at any time to bring it back exactly as it was, without a new review.
- You can't release new versions while it isn't shared.

A listing still waiting for review (or sent back with changes requested) can be **withdrawn** back to a draft instead, and a draft that was never shared can be deleted.

## Edit a listing

To change a live listing's name, description, icon, previews, categories, tags or
language, open it in the Marketplace and click **Edit listing** (only you, the
publisher, see this button).

- The same five-step wizard opens with every step **prefilled with what's live
  now**. Jump to any step with the numbered stepper.
- Nothing is saved while you edit. The last step, **Review changes**, lists which
  parts you changed and notes that a new version is snapshotted from your
  current workflow.
- **Submit update** sends everything in one go; **Discard changes** closes the
  wizard without touching the listing.

Every update goes to a quick moderation review. **The current version stays live
while it's reviewed**: the listing remains visible and installable in the
Marketplace, and your listing page shows **Update in review**. People who already
installed it keep their version either way.

- **Approved**: your changes (and the new version) replace the live listing.
- **Not approved**: the live version stays exactly as it was, and your listing
  page shows the moderator's note. Edit the listing again to submit a new update.

You'll get a notification either way. If you open **Edit listing** while an
update is still in review, the wizard continues from that update, and submitting
again replaces it.

## Explain the steps

Every step of a published workflow needs a short, plain-language **note**
(see [Notes & story](/app/workflows/narration/)) — what the step
does for the person running it, and why. People install what they understand.

The **Explain the steps** wizard step lists each step of your workflow in order,
with its note:

- A step counts as explained once its note has **at least 8 words**; it gets a
  green check.
- **✨ Draft with AI** writes a note for every step that doesn't have one yet,
  using your configured AI provider (or a local model). You can edit every draft.
- The notes are saved back onto the steps themselves, so the canvas **Story**
  tab and node notes show the same text.
- **Next** stays disabled until every step is explained — the counter next to it
  tells you how many notes are still missing.

See [Narration style](/app/workflows/narration-style/) for tips on
writing good notes.

## The "Narrated" badge

A flow where **every step has a note** earns a **✨ Narrated** badge on its
Marketplace card and listing — a signal that it teaches on open, not just runs.

## Review & publish

The last step shows your listing as people will see it, then what gets checked before you publish:

- **Secret check.** Every step's settings are scanned for anything that looks like a key, token or password. If something is found, publishing is blocked until you blank it or move it into a credential.
- **These values won't be shared.** Fields that are always left blank for installers, and other values you can choose to blank. Whoever installs the workflow fills them in.
- **Permissions & Safety.** What the workflow can access (for example, reading the active tab) and where it connects, worked out from its steps. Installers see the same summary before they install.

## How listings get discovered

Once approved, your listing can surface in a few places on the Marketplace:

- **Featured** — a hand-picked selection the AWFlow team highlights in the hero
  banner at the top of the Marketplace. Featuring is editorial: it isn't earned
  automatically, and there's nothing to opt into. Well-documented, reliable,
  clearly-scoped flows are the best candidates.
- **Trending now** — a rail showing flows that are **gaining installs this week**.
  This is computed automatically from recent install activity over a rolling
  window, so a burst of interest can put your flow here without any curation.
- **Top / search** — everywhere else, ordering blends rating, install count, and
  recency (with a **Trending** sort option on the search page if you want to see
  the same recent-activity view).

## Using shared workflows

- Open a listing in the Marketplace and click **Install**. Before anything is installed you see everything the workflow can do (*Made by* the author): the pages it reads or changes, the sites it reaches (named, or *To sites it finds while it runs*), and the connections it uses (*You pick yours after installing; none of the author's come with it*). **Before it runs** says it asks the first time it uses each site and each connection (you can tick **Always allow**), and lists the fields the author left blank that need you. You confirm it once. Sandboxed code shows as a calm grey *Runs code in a sandbox* row, not a warning.

- The listing shows **How it works** — a read-only preview of the steps (a list of steps in the side panel) — plus the apps it **works with** and how many setup steps it needs, so you know before installing.
- Prefer an editable copy? Click **Use as a template instead**. The copy keeps the access you approved; if you change it so it needs more, choose **Make it mine** to take responsibility for it — after that it runs freely, the author's updates no longer apply, and the credit ("Based on …") stays.
- If a workflow needs a newer AWFlow extension, the button says **Needs extension vX+**; update the extension first.
- After installing, the canvas opens with a **Ready to run** checklist: connect your own accounts and fill in the fields the author left blank, then test it.
- When the author releases a new version, you see **Update available** in your workflows list and on the listing, plus one notification. **Update** shows what's new and whether the new version needs more access; you only have to approve again if it does. Your settings carry over, and any new fields to fill are listed.
- If the author stops sharing it, your copy keeps working and shows "No longer listed". If AWFlow removes it for safety, it stops — see [Installed Marketplace Workflows](/app/troubleshooting/permissions-security/#installed-marketplace-workflows).

## Admin: maintaining templates in the canvas

Workspace admins curate the built-in Marketplace template library from **`#/admin/templates`**.

The templates list shows each template's title, category, price, and an **Editing** column that indicates when another admin currently has it open — hover it to see who and since when.

- **New template** opens a dialog to set a title, slug, summary, icon, category, price, sort order, and tags, then creates a draft and opens it directly in the canvas.
- Clicking a template opens it for editing on the same visual canvas used for regular workflows, plus a metadata panel for the fields above.
- Test-running a template while editing doesn't create an execution history entry — it's a scratch run, not a real automation execution.

### Edit locking

Only one admin can edit a given template at a time. When you open a template for editing, you acquire a lock that's automatically renewed while you stay on the page and released when you leave or save.

- If someone else is already editing, you'll see a **read-only** banner naming them and how long they've held the lock.
- **Force unlock** takes over the lock immediately — use it only when you're sure the other admin's session is stale, since it interrupts them and any of their unsaved changes are lost.
- If your own lock is taken over while you're mid-edit, a banner warns you that your changes since the last save weren't submitted, with a way to reacquire the lock or go back to the list.

## Admin: featuring a listing

Moderators can feature an **approved** listing from its review screen in the
moderation queue (**`#/admin/marketplace/moderation`**). Featuring is the
editorial lever behind the Marketplace **Featured** banner:

- **Feature / Unfeature** promotes or removes the listing from the Featured
  banner. Featured listings sort ahead of the normal ranking everywhere they
  appear.
- An optional **rank** orders featured listings relative to each other (lower
  numbers first); leave it blank to let them fall back to the normal score.
- Every feature/unfeature is written to the moderation **audit log** alongside
  approvals and rejections, so curation stays accountable.

**Trending** is not a moderator action — it's computed from recent installs and
needs no curation.
