Workflow tags let you label your workflows so you can organise and find them quickly. Tags are **per workflow**: each workflow keeps its own list of tag labels; there is no shared list of tags across workflows.

## Add or edit tags on a workflow

<StepCard n={1} title="Open the row menu">
  On the **Workflows** page, click the **⋮** at the start of the workflow's row.

  <StepShot shot="workflow-row-menu" spot="row-menu" n={1} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={2} title="Click Manage tags">
  <StepShot shot="workflow-row-menu" spot="manage-tags" n={2} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={3} title="Add or remove tags">
  In the **Manage tags** dialog, type a tag and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to add it. Click the **×** on a tag to remove it. A workflow can have as many tags as you like.

  <StepShot shot="workflow-tags" spot="add-tag" n={3} zoom={44} />
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={4} title="Click Save" check="the new tags show in the workflow's Tags column.">
  <StepShot shot="workflow-tags" spot="save" n={4} zoom={44} />
</StepCard>

You can also manage tags from the editor: the **⋮** menu at the top right of the canvas has the same **Manage tags** item.

> **Note:** Tags are also editable when you publish a workflow to the marketplace, from the publish form.

## Tag many workflows at once

Tick the checkbox of each workflow on the **Workflows** page, then click **Tags** in the toolbar that appears at the bottom. The dialog applies to every selected workflow:

## Filter the list by tag

On the **Workflows** page, open the **Tags** filter above the table. It only appears once at least one workflow has a tag.

  shot="workflows-filter"
