**Workflows** in the sidebar (or the bottom bar of the side panel) lists every workflow you have. Tap a workflow's card or name to open it. On a wide screen, double-clicking a row opens it too.

## The three tabs

- **Mine**: the workflows you created or imported.
- **Installed**: the workflows you installed from the Marketplace.
- **Runs**: every run of every workflow (see [Runs](/app/workflows/run-history/)). A badge counts the runs that need you.

**Add Workflow** (or **+ Create** in the sidebar) starts a new one.

## Find a workflow

- Type in the search box: it matches names and descriptions.
- Narrow the list with the filter chips: **Status** (*On* / *Off*), **Storage** (*Local* / *Cloud*), **Published**, **Needs setup** and **Tags**. See [Filter the list](/app/workflows/filters/).
- **Columns** shows or hides columns; click a header to sort.

Your search, filters and sort are kept in the page address, so a link or the back button brings them back, and they are remembered when you reopen the panel. **While you search, results come from every folder**, not only the one you have open. If nothing matches, **Clear** empties the search.

The list refreshes by itself when a workflow is saved or deleted in another AWFlow window.

A **Needs setup** badge next to a name means the workflow is missing something before it can run, such as a connection. Open it and look at **Ready to run**.

## Folders

Group workflows in **folders**, up to 3 levels deep, on this device and in your account.

- On a wide screen, the folder tree sits on the left with a count per folder; drag rows onto a folder to move them. **Hide folders** collapses it.
- In the side panel, folders appear first in the list, and **‹** goes back up.
- **New folder** creates one in the current folder; a folder's menu offers **Rename** and **Delete folder**. Deleting a folder moves its workflows up one level; it never deletes them.
- **Move to folder…** (row menu or bulk) opens a picker with **New folder**.
- A new workflow is created in the folder you have open. The editor's header shows the folder path.

Moving a workflow between this device and your account puts it at the top level, with a note.

## Turn a workflow On or Off

- **On**: it runs by itself when its trigger fires (a schedule, a page event, a shortcut…).
- **Off**: it only runs when you press **Run**.

Use the switch in the list or in the editor's header. A workflow whose only trigger is **When I press Run** has no switch: it shows **Runs when you press Run**. New workflows start **Off**: the first time you add an automatic trigger, the switch appears with a short tip.

## Actions on one workflow

Open the **⋮** menu of a row. In the editor, the **⋯** menu has most of the same items.

On **Mine**:

- **Open**, **Run** (if it has several triggers, you pick which one), **Improve with Aria**.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy (**Open copy** in the toast); **Edit** changes its name and description; **Manage tags**.
- **Workflow settings**: see [Workflow settings](/app/workflows/settings/).
- **Move to folder…**, **Move to cloud** / **Move to local** (signed-in users only, see below).
- **Upgrade workflow behaviour…** on older workflows: see [Workflow behaviour versions](/app/workflows/behaviour-versions/).
- **Publish to the marketplace** (or **Release** for a new version). See [Publishing](/app/workflows/publishing/).
- **Export** downloads it as a file. See [Export & import](/app/workflows/export-import/).
- **Archive** (see below) and **Delete…**.

On **Installed**: **Open**, **Run**, **Export**, **Update to v…** when a newer version exists, **Checkout a version**, **Reset permissions** (it asks for site and app access again next time) and **Remove**. Their status column says when the author released an update, stopped sharing it, or AWFlow is reviewing or removed it.

## Archive instead of delete

**Archive** puts a workflow away without losing anything:

- it is switched **Off**, and hidden from the list, the step picker, the command palette and Aria's mentions;
- its versions and runs are kept.

The toast offers **Undo**, which puts it back as it was (On again if it was On). See archived workflows with the **Archived** chip or the **Archived** entry of the folder tree. An archived workflow opens **read-only**: **Unarchive** to edit or run it.

**Delete…** asks first and lists what goes with the workflow: its run history, its marketplace page, its stored data. It offers **Archive instead**.

## Local or cloud storage

Every workflow is stored in one of two places:

- **Local** (*On this device*): in your browser only. No step limit, but local workflows can't be published.
- **Cloud** (*In your account*): it follows you when you sign in elsewhere, and counts towards your [plan's limits](/app/settings/plan/).

You need to be signed in to move a workflow. A move copies it, checks the copy, then deletes the original; if anything fails, the original stays. **Undo** in the toast moves it back.

<Aside type="caution">
Only the current version moves. Its [version history](/app/workflows/history/) stays behind. Moving to the cloud can fail if you have reached your plan's limits.
</Aside>

Bulk actions on **Mine**:

- **Export**: secrets are always left out ("Passwords and keys were left out").
- **To cloud** / **To local**: workflows already there are skipped. Signed-in users only.
- **Turn on** / **Turn off**.
- **Move to folder…**, **Archive** / **Unarchive**.
- **Tags**: **Add tags** and **Remove tags** across the selection. See [Workflow tags](/app/workflows/tags/#tag-many-workflows-at-once).
- **Delete**: after you confirm. If the workflows have their own chat memory, you choose whether to delete those conversations or keep them in [Memory](/app/memory/overview/).

Turning on or off, tagging, duplicating and moving storage can be undone from the toast.

On **Installed**: **Export** and **Remove** (it also clears the permissions you granted them).
