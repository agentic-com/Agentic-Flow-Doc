Clicking the AWFlow icon in your browser toolbar opens the app in Chrome's **side panel**, next to the page you're working on. You can keep the page and your workflows side by side: pick elements, run a workflow, and watch its results without switching tabs.

The side panel starts narrow (about 320–450px), and you can drag its left edge to make it wider. The app adapts to whatever width you give it.

## Opening it

- Click the **AWFlow** icon in the toolbar. If you don't see it, open the puzzle-piece **Extensions** menu and pin it.
- Click the icon again, or use the panel's close button, to hide it. Your work stays where you left it.

## What changes when the panel is narrow

To fit the panel, the app rearranges itself instead of cutting things off:

- **Bottom bar.** On the main pages, a bar at the bottom takes you to **Home**, **Aria**, **Workflows**, **Marketplace** and **More** (the full navigation). It hides inside a workflow, in an open chat and while the on-screen keyboard is up.
- **Header.** It shows the name of the page you're on (the same name as in the navigation), a **search** icon (the same search as <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>), and the inbox (notifications and runs waiting for you, with one count). On pages deeper in, **‹** goes back to the page you came from.
- **Toasts and bars** stack above the bottom bar and the step sheet's buttons, never on top of them.
- **Lists** (workflows, connections, knowledge bases, [memory](/app/memory/overview/), published workflows). Each item is a compact two-line row with its status and a **⋯** menu. The **New** button is always visible next to the tabs.
- **Forms** (new connection, new knowledge base…). They open as a full-height sheet, with the Save/Create button pinned to the bottom.
- **Workflow canvas.**
  - The **Editor / Logs / Runs** switch gets its own row under the header.
  - The canvas tools sit in a single vertical rail.
  - The step picker opens as a sheet from the bottom.
  - Step settings open as a full-height sheet, with an **Input · Setup · Output** switch that shows one pane at a time.
  - The Aria dock opens as a full-width sheet with **Back to canvas**.
  - The minimap is hidden until the panel is wide enough.
- **Runs.** Tap a run in the list to open its details, and use **‹ Runs** to go back.
- **Settings.** **Settings** shows a list of sections with their current state (for example "Usage stats on"). Tap one to open it, and use **‹ Settings** to go back.
- **Aria.** Your chat history opens as a drawer from the history icon.
- **Agents.** **Try it**, **Test a rule** and **Recent runs** open from a button at the bottom, in a sheet that keeps your test chat when you close it.
- **Marketplace.** On a workflow's page, the **Install** button stays pinned at the bottom of the panel while you read about it.

## Open in full tab

In the extension, the **Open in full tab** button in the header (or the canvas **⋯** menu) opens the same page in an AWFlow tab, reusing one if it's already open; unsaved canvas changes come along. From the tab, **Dock to side panel** brings you back.

The app also remembers where you were: closing the panel and opening it again brings back the last page, an unsaved canvas (with **Restore** / **Discard**), its zoom and the step you had open.

## Making it wider

Drag the panel's left edge to widen it. At around **450px**, headers fit on one line. From around **670px**, full tables, the canvas minimap, split views and side-by-side layouts come back, as in a regular tab.
