You don't have to open the knowledge bases page to add something. There are three shortcuts, and they all use the same small picker:

- the knowledge bases **pinned in the current chat** come first and are preselected;
- each one shows its size and privacy ("12 sources", "on this device only");
- knowledge bases built with a cloud embedding model can't be picked here;
- **New knowledge base…** creates one and selects it.

Click **Save and index**. Indexing runs in the background like any other source (see [Add sources](/app/knowledge-bases/create/#add-sources)); a notice says **Added to *Product handbook* · 24 chunks** with **Open** when it is done.

Saving the same file, page or URL again **replaces** the older copy instead of adding a second one.

## From a chat attachment

Files, PDFs, tabs and selections you attach in a chat are normally read for that one message. To keep one:

1. In the composer's attachment tray, open the attachment's **⋯** menu.
2. Choose **Save to knowledge base…**.

A page is read again from its tab; a selection is saved as "*Page title* (selection)". Screenshots and images have no text, so they can't be saved.

## From the right-click menu (extension)

On any web page, right-click and choose:

- **AWFlow › Add page to knowledge base** — saves the text of the page. It is read from the tab, so pages you are signed in to work too.
- **AWFlow › Add selection to knowledge base** — saves the text you selected.

AWFlow opens with the picker already filled in.

## Ask Aria or an agent

Just ask: "Add this page to my product handbook", or "Save this text to the Support macros knowledge base". Aria and your agents can add the current page, a URL or a piece of text to a knowledge base you name.

Adding to a knowledge base is a **write**, so it follows the agent's [rules](/app/chat-and-agents/browser-control/): it **asks you every time** unless you answer **Always**. When it is done, the reply says what happened, for example "Added *Refund policy · Help Center* to Product handbook · 24 chunks · replaced the older copy."

This works in chats with Aria and with an agent. Agents in a team thread can't add to a knowledge base.
