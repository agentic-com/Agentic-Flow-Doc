**Activity** shows what your [agents](/app/chat-and-agents/agents/) and [teams](/app/chat-and-agents/teams/) did: their answers, routine runs, team hand-offs and the actions waiting for your approval. Open it from the sidebar under **Automate › Activity**.

Activity is about agents. For the runs of a workflow, open that workflow's [run history](/app/workflows/run-history/).

## Read the feed

Entries are grouped by day (*Today*, *Yesterday*, then older dates). Each one tells you who did what, for example:

- an agent **answered** in a chat, with the number of steps and whether it used an on-device or a cloud model,
- an agent **ran a routine**,
- a team ran or handed work from one member to another,
- an agent is **waiting for approval**, or **skipped an action** that needs your OK.

Entries that need you carry a **Needs you** tag and a **Review** button. Other entries have an **Open** link that takes you to the chat or team thread.

## Filter the feed

The left column lets you narrow the list:

- **All agents** shows everything.
- Pick one agent to see only its work.
- **Needs you** shows only what is waiting for you, with a count.

When nothing is waiting, the **Needs you** view reads *Nothing needs you right now.*

## Routines

On wide screens, the right column lists your agents' **routines**. Each has an on/off switch and a short description of when it runs. Click a routine to open it in its agent's **Routines** tab.

- **Show it once** records what you do and saves it as a routine for Aria.
- **Turn a chat into a routine** opens Aria's **Routines** tab.

See [Agents › Routines](/app/chat-and-agents/agents/) for how routines work.

## Where activity is kept

Activity is stored **on this device only** and is not synced. The newest 500 entries are kept; older ones drop off automatically.
