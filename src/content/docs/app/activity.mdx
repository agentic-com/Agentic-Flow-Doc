In AWFlow 1.0, the **Activity** page is part of **Workflows › Runs**: one list for everything that ran, workflows and [agents](/app/chat-and-agents/agents/) alike. Old links to Activity open it.

## What your agents did

**Workflows › Runs** lists, newest first and grouped by day (*Today*, *Yesterday*, then older dates):

- the runs of your workflows (see [Runs](/app/workflows/run-history/)),
- what your agents and [teams](/app/chat-and-agents/teams/) did: an agent **answered** in a chat (with the number of steps, on-device or cloud model), **ran a routine**, a team handed work from one member to another,
- what is **waiting for you**: approvals and forms, listed first.

Filter it with **Needs you**, **Failed**, **Running** or **All**, and with **Workflow or agent** to see one agent only. Entries that need you carry a **Review** button; others open the chat or team thread.

## Routines

The **Workflow or agent** sheet lists each agent's **routines**, with an on/off switch and when it runs. It works at every width, including the side panel.

- **Show it once** records what you do and saves it as a routine for Aria.
- **Turn a chat into a routine** opens Aria's **Routines** tab.
