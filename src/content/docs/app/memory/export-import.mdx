## Export

Memory is exported as an **`.awmem`** file:

- **Export all** (top of the Memory page) saves every conversation and fact on this device.
- **Export** on a conversation, a selection or an owner's facts saves just those.
- Tick **Protect with a passphrase** to encrypt the file. Use at least the minimum length shown. You need the passphrase to import it, and it can't be recovered if you forget it.

## Import

**Import** opens an `.awmem` file. If it's protected, enter its passphrase. A preview shows how many conversations, messages and facts it holds and when it was exported; untick what you don't want.

<GuidedShot
  shot="memory-import"
  steps={[
    { spot: 'preview', title: 'Check what the file holds', body: 'Untick **conversations** or **facts** to leave them out.' },
    { spot: 'import', title: 'Click Import', check: 'the conversations appear in **Memory › Conversations** and the facts under **Facts**.' },

Import **never overwrites anything**: every conversation is added as a copy, and facts you already have are skipped. Use it to move memory to another browser or device, or to keep a copy before clearing.

## When you delete an agent, a team or a workflow

- **Agent or team**: the delete dialog asks **Also delete its N memories** (on by default) and shows one of them. Uncheck it to keep them: they appear under **Facts › No owner**. An agent's chats stay in **Memory › Conversations**.
- **Workflow**: if it has its own chat memory, the dialog offers **Delete its conversations** (the default) or **Keep it in Memory**, with the number of messages and the size. Conversations under a **shared** key, and conversations another workflow also uses, are always kept, and the dialog says so.
- **Workflow with canvas dock chats**: its saved dock chats are part of its conversations and are offered for deletion with it.
