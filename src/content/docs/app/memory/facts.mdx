Open **Memory › Facts**: every fact on this device, grouped by who remembers it. **Remembered by**, on the left, lists each owner and how many facts it has:

- **Aria**, your assistant;
- each **agent**, **team** and **project** that has facts;
- **No owner**: facts whose agent, team or project was deleted while you chose to keep its memories. Delete them or export a copy.

For each fact you see where it came from ("Learned in *Trip to Lisbon*" or *You added this*), when, when it was **last used**, and **Shared with *team*** when an agent shared it with a team.

## Add, edit or forget a fact

- **Add a fact** to the selected owner, **edit** a fact, or **forget** it (with **Undo**).
- A fact can be up to **500 characters**, so it can be found and fits in the model's context. A hint counts the characters as you type.
- **Pinned · always included** marks a pinned fact: it's sent with every message. You can't change the pin here.
- **Export** saves the owner's facts as an `.awmem` file. **Search facts** appears when an owner has more than six.

## Suggested facts

At the top of the tab, **Suggested** lists facts your assistant **noticed** in your chats and is waiting for you to confirm. Nothing there is used until you choose **Remember** (or **Edit** it first). **Dismiss** throws it away. Unanswered suggestions are removed after **14 days**. See [Remember this?](/app/chat-and-agents/memory/#remember-this)
