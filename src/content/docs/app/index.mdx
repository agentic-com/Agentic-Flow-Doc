## Home

**Home** is your **Today** page:

- **Needs you**: runs waiting for an approval or a form, workflows that failed in the last 24 hours, and setup that is missing (up to 4, with **See all** in Runs).
- **Continue**: the last workflow and the last chat you opened.
- **Your automations**: the workflows that are **On**, with their last run, their next scheduled run and a 14-day sparkline.
- One suggested **next step**, a **Create** row (New workflow, Ask Aria, from a template, blank, import), and a small usage line linked to your plan.
- The **insights** recap: runs, failure rate, typical run time and time saved over the period you pick (see [Insights](/app/insights/)). A **Critical · Health** chip appears when [Health](/app/settings/health/) finds something serious, and an update card when an AWFlow update touches your workflows (see [Updating AWFlow](/app/updating-awflow/)).

Under it, **Discover**:

- Category chips that open the Marketplace, and **one featured** workflow (an editor's pick that works with your apps, else one made for you, else the top trending).
- **Top workflows**: *Works with your apps* (no setup or few connections first), *Trending this week* and *New from the community*. Each card says how much setup it needs ("No setup", "~2 min · OpenAI key", "~10 min · Google account"); tap the chip for the details.
- **Tutorials**: continue where you stopped, by level.
- **News**: the latest release and announcements, with a dot on what you haven't seen.

The tab you pick is remembered. Offline, Discover shows what it cached, without images.

The very first time, Home shows **See it work in 5 seconds** with one **Run it** button, plus **Start from a template** and **Describe it to Aria**. After your first own change works, AWFlow says so once.

## Find anything

<kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> (or the search button in the header) opens the command palette: workflows, chats, agents, knowledge bases and connections by name, actions (**New workflow**, **New chat**, **New agent**, **Add connection**, **Import a workflow**), your recent pages, and **Ask Aria: …** at the end of every search. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/app/workflows/keyboard-shortcuts/).

On a wide screen, the sidebar can fold into an icon rail, and **+ Create** offers the same new-item actions.
