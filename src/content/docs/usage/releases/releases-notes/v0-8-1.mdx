Published: **October 7, 2026**

## Highlights

Version 0.8.1 adds **knowledge bases**: collections of your own documents,
pages and notes that Aria, your agents and your workflows can search, all on
your device. It also rebuilds the AI harness that runs every assistant reply,
so you can see what Aria is doing, stop it cleanly, and trust what it builds.
A batch of fixes makes agents and teams more dependable.

## Knowledge bases

A knowledge base holds sources you add (files, web pages, notes) and makes them
searchable by meaning and by keyword. Everything is stored and searched in the
browser.

- A **Knowledge bases home** where you create a base with its sources, and an
  **Add sources** dialog for adding more later.
- A **detail page** per base: test a search, open a source to see its content,
  see what a delete will affect before you confirm, and re-embed after
  changing the embedding model.
- **Save to a knowledge base from anywhere** — from a page, a selection, or a
  chat.
- **Hybrid search** combines keyword (BM25) and vector search, with optional
  **on-device reranking** for better ordering. Search settings can be tuned
  per base and per tool.
- **Portable `.awkb` files** let you export a base and import it on another
  device. Importing a workflow lets you map its knowledge-base references to
  your own bases.
- PDF sources keep their page numbers, so citations point to the right page.
- One vocabulary and **one privacy control** for knowledge across the app.
- There is **no plan limit** on knowledge bases: they live on your device.

Encrypted account backups of knowledge bases were built but are **switched off
for now**. Use `.awkb` export to keep a copy.

### Knowledge in chats, agents and workflows

- A **knowledge picker** in chat lets you choose which bases Aria can search.
  Answers show **citations you can open**, and the search steps explain
  themselves.
- Agents get a **Knowledge** tab and a `search_knowledge` tool, and agent
  knowledge works on every surface where the agent runs.
- **Project files** now use the same knowledge engine.
- Workflow knowledge nodes gained a device-only guard, fenced context, source
  details, MMR and metadata filters, a search tool, hybrid search, and a richer
  base picker. Workflows remember which base they came from.

## The AI harness

The engine behind every assistant reply was reworked end to end, covering how
it runs, the context it sends, model providers, observability, and the chat UI:

- A **work timeline** shows each model and tool step as it happens, and a
  **reply receipt** sums up what a reply used.
- **Stopping a reply** is clean, and you are told when a step limit is
  reached, when Auto falls back to another model, or when a skill is off.
- **Turn traces** (Developer mode) show where the time and tokens of each reply
  went, with JSON export. Traces stay on your device.
- The chat UI is built from **Svelte AI Elements** components (shimmer,
  chain of thought, context usage, confirmations, plans).
- Generated node parameters are **validated against each node's schema**
  before a workflow is applied.
- **Gemini Nano** (Chrome AI) can call tools through the prompt, and local
  models **stream replies token by token** even when tools are bound.
- A clarifying question asked during a build **survives a page reload**.

## Agents and teams fixes

- Workflow and MCP tools are locked by **what they do**, not by their name.
- Agent approvals are **queued**, so approving one never declines another.
- Aria keeps her skills inside **team threads**.
- Cloud tokens are metered **per model call**, and the limit is enforced during
  a turn rather than after it.
- A team thread cut off by closing the app is **shown and can be retried**.
- `delegate_to` always has its schema, and **team-scoped memories** are shared
  between team members.
- Approval activity titles are translated again.

## Fixes and localization

- All local database connections share one lock, which fixes hangs when
  several features open the database at once.
- Knowledge base strings are translated into French, German and Chinese, with
  translation-safe plurals.
