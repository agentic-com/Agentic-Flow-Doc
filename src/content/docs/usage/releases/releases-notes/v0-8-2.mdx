Published: **October 10, 2026**

## Highlights

Version 0.8.2 is about **memory**: what AWFlow remembers, how it uses it, and
how you see and manage it. Chats, workflow conversations and the facts your
agents learn now live in one store on your device, with one **Memory** page to
read, search, export and clean them up. Facts are suggested before they are
saved and picked by relevance. Workflows get one **Chat Memory** node instead
of three. A new **Settings › Storage** page shows everything AWFlow keeps in
your browser.

The **Marketplace** also gets a large safety and quality pass: installs you can
update in one click, listings you can stop sharing, a safety kill switch,
access checked on your device, a stronger secret scan, and isolated moderation
test runs. Agent and team templates get the same care.

## The Memory page

**Chat memories** becomes **Memory** (old links redirect), with three tabs:

- **Conversations** — every chat, quick chat, team thread, workflow
  conversation and canvas dock chat, with filters by kind, **search inside
  messages** with snippets that open the conversation at the right message,
  and size badges for anything over 1 MB.
- A **transcript page** for each conversation: its summary, tool steps,
  interrupted replies, which workflows use it, what takes its space, and
  **Export**, **Slim**, **Clear messages** and **Delete**.
- **Facts** — every fact by owner, a **Suggested** queue, and **No owner** for
  facts kept after their agent or team was deleted.
- **Storage** — what memory takes and what is easy to free: **slim**,
  **archive** or **delete** conversations and **clear orphans**, always with
  the space freed shown first.
- **`.awmem` export and import**, with an optional passphrase. Import always
  adds copies and never overwrites.
- Workflow conversations written by the old Persistent Chat Memory node, which
  were never visible before, now appear marked **Recovered**.

## Storage

- **Settings › Storage** shows everything AWFlow keeps in the browser — local
  models, memory, knowledge bases, workflows and run history — against what the
  browser allows.
- **Keep my data** asks the browser not to delete AWFlow data when the disk is
  low.
- A one-time suggestion when memory passes **500 MB**, **1 GB**, or the browser
  is **80% full**.
- An opt-in **automatic clean-up** rule for conversations not opened for a
  number of days (slim, archive or delete). It is off by default.

## Smarter facts

- **Remember this?** — preferences the assistant notices are suggested, and
  saved only when you confirm. "Remember that…" still saves at once, and
  questions are never saved.
- **Relevance recall** — each message uses the facts most relevant to it, found
  by a small on-device model that downloads on first use. Until then, the
  newest facts are used.
- The context meter shows **how many facts** went in and whether memory search
  is ready. Facts are sent as reference data, never as instructions, and no
  longer push the conversation out of the context window.
- Facts longer than 500 characters are refused with a clear hint instead of
  being lost.
- The agent that answered (or that you @mentioned) learns; an agent with
  learning off never learns, even in a project.
- Share a fact with **one specific team**. A team's memory tab lists what
  members shared, with **Stop sharing**.
- Deleting an agent or a team asks whether to delete its memories too.

## Long chats

- **View summary** opens the rolling summary to read and edit it, or turn it
  into fact suggestions. **Compact now** summarises early on demand.
- Context windows come from a list of known models; unknown models assume a
  safe 8k and the meter marks it as estimated.
- A reply cut off by closing the tab is kept as **interrupted**, with
  **Retry**.
- The notch quick chat now trims and summarises long chats, and uses and
  suggests memories like the full chat.
- **Canvas dock** chats are saved with their workflow and reopen next time,
  with a **Saved chats** list.
- Team members who are handed work see the thread's summary and last turns.

## Workflow memory

- One **Chat Memory** node replaces Local Memory, Persistent Chat Memory and
  Window Buffer Memory. Storage: a conversation, a key, or this run only. Keys
  are scoped to the workflow by default, or shared. History fits the model's
  window (or the last N messages), older messages can be summarised, and tool
  results can be remembered.
- Existing workflows keep working and are upgraded to Chat Memory when saved.
- The picked conversation is cleared when a workflow is published or shared.
- Deleting a workflow offers to delete its own conversations; shared keys are
  kept.

## Plans

Conversations are **no longer limited by plans**: they are stored on your
device.

## Encryption

With **Local secret protection** on, conversation messages, summaries and
facts are encrypted on this device. Titles and sizes stay visible; unlock to
read. Unlocking in the app also unlocks the notch for the session. While
protection is on, search covers titles only and chats use your pinned and
newest facts.

## Marketplace

### Installing and updating

- Listing buttons show where you stand: **Install**, **Installing…**,
  **Open** with the installed version, **Update to vN**, **Manage listing** for
  your own listings, or **Needs extension vX+**.
- **Update available** appears on the Installed tab, on listing pages and on
  search cards. **Update** shows the release notes since your version, what
  access changes, and which settings carry over. You only confirm again when
  the update needs more access; otherwise you can also **Stay on vN**.
- **Make it mine** turns an installed workflow into your own copy, credited to
  the original. When a step is blocked, you can make it yours or remove that
  step.
- Listings show a **read-only preview of the workflow**, **Works with** chips
  and the number of setup steps. Installs are all or nothing: a failed install
  leaves nothing half-created.
- **What it can access** is now checked on your device from the version's own
  steps, and the page tells you when the author's list was incomplete. More
  nodes that reach the network (AI providers, MCP tools, RSS, web search,
  notifications and others) are now listed, and MCP servers are approved
  before an agent connects to them.
- An installed workflow whose steps did not change is no longer blocked just
  because AWFlow now classifies a node differently. New access is asked for at
  run time instead.
- Running an installed workflow no longer fails with "its steps changed since
  you approved it" after a previous run wrote a URL in its output.
- Marketplace pages load faster: the home page in one go, paged category,
  author and request board pages, and search that always shows the latest
  results.

### Publishing and sharing

- **Stop sharing** removes a listing from the Marketplace while people who
  installed it keep their copy. Use **Share again** to bring it back,
  **Withdraw** for a listing still in review, and **Delete draft** for drafts.
- The **secret scan** catches more kinds of keys, tokens and webhook URLs,
  re-runs every time you tick **Blank for installers**, and marks each fix
  **✓ Fixed**. Values AWFlow blanked automatically are shown to you.
- Errors from the server are shown clearly: the exact step and field that look
  like a secret, or which listing details are still missing, with a link to
  that step.
- Marketplace notifications have their own icons and open the right place,
  for example the edit wizard when a listing needs changes.

### Safety

- If AWFlow removes a listing for safety, installed copies **stop running**,
  their triggers are switched off, and you see a banner explaining why with
  **Remove**. Listings under review show an amber notice instead.

## Agent and team templates

- Before installing, a **What it can do** panel lists the template's tools,
  workflows, hand-offs, knowledge search and routines, and asks you to confirm
  when it can act on pages or run workflows.
- Installs are all or nothing, and the template page shows **Installed · Open**
  once you have it.
- Authors can update a template (the approved version stays live during
  review), **Remove from marketplace** and **Share again**.

Relevant docs:

- [Agent & team templates](/usage/using-the-app/chat-and-agents/templates/)

## For moderators

- Moderation decisions are tied to the **exact version you reviewed**. If the
  author changes it meanwhile, your decision is not saved and you can load the
  new version.
- Approving uses a checklist; bulk approve is limited to AWFlow's official
  imports, and rejecting or suspending always needs a reason.
- **Test runs are isolated**: no saved accounts, page steps run in a fresh test
  tab, every site asks first, and a panel lists the network activity.
- The review page flags when a listing declares less access than its steps
  need.
- A new **Marketplace › Insights** page shows the install funnel, declined
  permissions and where installs come from, based on anonymous usage events
  you can opt out of.
- The audit log is paged and can be filtered by action, person, target and
  date.

## Fixes and polish

- **Faster sign-in**: AWFlow no longer requests an extra token at sign-in, and
  changing your password works on Firefox.
- A new **logo** across the app icons, sidebar and loading screen.
- Marketplace and memory strings are translated into French, German and
  Chinese, with no English fragments left in translated messages.
