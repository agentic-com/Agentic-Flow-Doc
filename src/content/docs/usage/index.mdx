Agentic WorkFlow lets you build powerful, browser-based automations that can interact with any web page, process information with AI, and save your results locally — all from a simple, visual interface.

No setup. No coding. Just drag, connect, and run.

## How It Works

Agentic WorkFlow runs entirely inside your browser for speed, privacy, and direct web page access.

**1. Visual Workflow Builder** – Create workflows by dragging and connecting "nodes" (actions and triggers)

**2. Browser Integration** – Direct access to web pages for real-time interaction and data extraction

**3. AI Integration** – Connect to local LLMs or cloud AI services for intelligent processing

**4. Execution Engine** – Run workflows manually, on triggers, or in production mode

**5. Sharing & Collaboration** – Export workflows and share with your team or community
