The **Credentials** page lets you narrow the list to the credentials you care about. The filter sits in the toolbar above the table, next to the search box. Your selection — along with sorting, visible columns and rows-per-page — is **remembered** and re-applied automatically the next time you open the page.

## Filter by storage

The **Storage** filter narrows the list by where each credential is saved:

- **Cloud** — saved to your account.
- **Local** — saved on this device only.

1. On the **Credentials** page, open the **Storage** filter above the table.
2. Select **Cloud**, **Local**, or both. A count badge shows how many values are active, and the table updates immediately.
3. Use **Clear filters** to reset.

You can also use the search box to match credentials by name.

## Filters are remembered

Your filter, sort order, chosen columns and page size are saved in the browser. When you reload the app or come back later, the list opens exactly as you left it.

> **Note:** This is stored on the device you are using, so it does not follow you to another browser or device. Clearing your browser data resets it to the defaults.
