AWFlow keeps your data **in your browser**: chats, memory, knowledge bases, on-device models and workflows. **Settings › Storage** shows what it all takes and helps you keep it safe and small. Nothing on this page is uploaded.

## What takes the space

The top card shows the total AWFlow uses, **out of about how much this browser allows**, with a bar split by category:

| Category | What it counts | Manage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Local models** | Models downloaded for on-device AI | [Local AI](/usage/using-the-app/local-models/) |
| **Memory** | Conversations and facts, with how much it grew **this week** | [Memory → Storage](/usage/using-the-app/memory/overview/#storage-tab) |
| **Knowledge bases** | Bases and their chunks | [Knowledge bases](/usage/using-the-app/knowledge-bases/overview/) |
| **Workflows & run history** | Your workflows and their past runs | [Workflows](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/create/) |
| **Other** | Everything else AWFlow stores | — |

The total and the limit are **estimated by the browser**; the size of each category is AWFlow's own measure. Some browsers don't report a limit: then only AWFlow's measures are shown.

## Keep my data

When the disk runs low, a browser may delete a site's data on its own — for AWFlow that means chats, memory, knowledge bases and models. The **Protected from automatic clean-up** card tells you whether your data is safe from that:

- **Not protected** — click **Keep my data** to ask the browser to keep it. The browser decides; most grant it to an installed extension or a site you use often.
- **Protected** — the browser won't delete AWFlow data when the disk is low.

Clearing the browser's site data still removes everything, protected or not. Use **Export memory** (and [knowledge base export](/usage/using-the-app/knowledge-bases/export-import-backup/)) to keep a copy of what matters.

## When memory gets large

With **Tell me when memory gets large** on (the default), AWFlow lets you know once when memory passes **500 MB**, again at **1 GB**, and when the browser is **80% full**:

- a short notice in the app, and
- a banner on **Settings › Storage** and **Memory → Storage**, for example *Memory passed 500 MB — Slimming 12 old chats would free about 340 MB. Nothing you read is lost.*

**Review** opens Memory → Storage filtered to what is **easy to free**. **Not now** hides the banner until the next threshold. Turn the switch off to stop these suggestions.

## Automatic clean-up

**Clean up old conversations** is **off by default**: nothing is removed unless you turn it on. When you do, set the rule:

- **When a conversation hasn't been opened for** *N* days (90 by default),
- **slim it**, **archive it (export, then remove)** or **delete it**,
- **Applies to**: **Aria chats**, **Quick chats** and/or **Workflow conversations** (Aria and quick chats by default),
- **Skip pinned chats** (on by default).

A live line says what the rule would do **right now** ("Right now this would slim 8 conversations and free ≈ 120 MB"), with **See which** to list them. **Run now** applies it immediately, and **History** lists the last runs and what each freed.

The rule runs once a day when AWFlow starts. In the browser extension it also runs **in the background**, once a day, even when AWFlow isn't open.

:::note[Archive needs AWFlow open]
Archiving saves an `.awmem` file to your computer, so it only runs while AWFlow is open. Slim and delete also run in the background.
:::

See [Slim, archive and delete](/usage/using-the-app/memory/overview/#slim-archive-and-delete) for what each action keeps.
