# Memory

**Memory** shows everything AWFlow remembers, in one place. Open **Memory** in the sidebar, under **Your data**.

AWFlow remembers three kinds of things:

| What | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| **Chats** | Your conversations with Aria, your agents and your teams: the Aria page, quick chats from the notch and the right-click card, team threads and chats in the canvas dock. |
| **Workflow conversations** | The chat history of workflows that use a [Chat Memory](/nodes/builtin/ai/aidependencies/chatmemories/chatmemory/) node, such as a chatbot built with a [Chat Trigger](/nodes/builtin/trigger/chat/). |
| **Facts** | Short things your assistant and your agents learned about you ("Prefers short answers"), or that you added yourself. See [Assistant memory](/usage/using-the-app/chat-and-agents/memory/). |

Everything is stored **only on this device**, in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

:::note[Chat memories is now Memory]
The page used to be called **Chat memories** and listed only workflow conversations. It now lists everything. Old links to the Chat memories page open Memory.
:::

At the top of the page:

- a **storage strip** with what memory takes on this device, split into **Chats**, **Workflow** and **Facts**, and a **Manage space →** link to the [Storage tab](#storage-tab);
- **Import** and **Export all** (see [Export and import](#export-and-import)).

The page has three tabs: **Conversations**, **Facts** and **Storage**.

## Conversations tab

Every conversation of every kind, newest first.

- **Filter by kind** with the chips: **All**, **Aria chats**, **Quick chat**, **Teams**, **Workflows** and **Canvas dock**. Each chip shows how many conversations it has; kinds you have none of are hidden.
- Under each title, a line says who or what the conversation belongs to: an agent and a project ("Writer · Trip to Lisbon"), a team, or a workflow and its key ("Workflow · Support bot · key inbox · shared").
- **Size** appears only when a conversation is large: a badge shows above **1 MB**. Sort by **Size** to find the biggest ones, or open the [Storage tab](#storage-tab).
- A **Recovered** badge marks an old workflow conversation that was saved but never shown before (written by the former Persistent Chat Memory node). It is now visible, and the workflow keeps using it.

Each row's **⋯** menu has **Open**, **Export**, **Clear messages** and **Delete**. Select several rows to **Export** or **Delete** them together.

### Search inside conversations

The search box looks in **titles and in the messages themselves**. Matching messages are listed per conversation, with a short snippet and your words highlighted. Click a match to open the conversation **at that message**: it is scrolled into view and highlighted, with **Load older messages** and **Load newer messages** around it.

Search runs on a small word index kept on this device. After an update, older messages are indexed in the background; meanwhile the results say **Indexing older messages…**. Tool results, reasoning and attachments are not searched.

## A conversation's page

Click a conversation to read it. Older messages load as you scroll up.

- **Summary** — when a long chat was summarised to fit the model, a notice says **18 earlier messages summarised · the agent sees this summary instead of them**. Click **View summary** to read it, with the messages it covers, when it was updated, and *edited by you* if you changed it (see [Long chats](/usage/using-the-app/chat-and-agents/chat/#long-chats-and-the-summary)).
- **Tool steps** — tools the agent used are shown in place. Click one to see its arguments and its result as stored. A result that was [slimmed](#slim-archive-and-delete) shows only its one-line summary.
- **Interrupted replies** — a reply that was cut off (the tab closed while it was being written) is kept with what had been written, marked as interrupted.

The side panel shows:

- **Used by** — the workflows that use this conversation, with a link to each. Deleting the conversation clears the memory of those workflows: they start fresh on their next run.
- **What takes the space** — a bar and a list of what the conversation holds: text, tool results, reasoning, unchosen regenerated answers, build snapshots, attachments and its share of the search index.
- **Details** — created, last used, size, *On this device*, and for an old workflow conversation its former number.

The header has **Export**, **Slim**, **Clear messages** and **Delete**.

- **Clear messages** removes every message but keeps the conversation, so the workflows and chats that use it keep working and start fresh.
- **Delete** removes the conversation and its messages. It can't be undone — export it first to keep a copy.

### When a conversation is in use

If a workflow uses the conversation you are about to delete, the dialog lists those workflows (with **Open** for each) and explains that they will start with an empty memory next time they run. Choose **Clear messages only** to keep the conversation and drop its messages instead.

## Facts tab

Every fact on this device, grouped by who remembers it. The list on the left, **Remembered by**, shows each owner and how many facts it has:

- **Aria** — your assistant;
- each **agent**, **team** and **project** that has facts;
- **No owner** — facts whose agent, team or project was deleted while you chose to keep its memories. Delete them or export a copy.

For each fact you see where it came from ("Learned in *Trip to Lisbon*" or *You added this*), when, when it was **last used**, and **Shared with *team*** when an agent shared it with a team.

- **Add a fact** to the selected owner, **edit** a fact or **forget** it (with **Undo**). A fact can be up to **500 characters**: facts are short so they can be found and fit in the model's context. A hint counts the characters as you type.
- **Pinned · always included** marks a pinned fact. Pinned facts are always sent with your messages; you can't change the pin here.
- **Export** saves the owner's facts as an `.awmem` file.
- **Search facts** appears when an owner has more than six.

### Suggested facts

At the top of the tab, **Suggested** lists facts your assistant **noticed** in your chats and is waiting for you to confirm. Nothing there is used until you choose **Remember** (or **Edit** it first). **Dismiss** throws it away. Unanswered suggestions are removed after **14 days**. See [Remember this?](/usage/using-the-app/chat-and-agents/memory/#remember-this)

## Storage tab

The Storage tab shows how much space memory takes **in this browser** and helps you free it.

- A **bar** splits memory by content: text, tool results, reasoning, regenerated answers, build snapshots, attachments, search index.
- Four **tiles**:
  - **Aria & quick chats** — size, number of chats and the share taken by tool results;
  - **Workflow conversations** — they grow with every run;
  - **Facts** — and how many have no owner;
  - **Easy to free** — what slimming and clearing orphans would free right now.
- **Filters**: **Largest first**, **Easy to free**, **Not opened in 90 days**, **No owner** and **Workflow deleted**.
- A table lists each conversation with its **size**, **what's in it**, when it was **last opened** and what it **can free**.

Select conversations to **Slim**, **Archive** or **Delete** them; each button shows the space it frees. **Clear orphans** appears when there are conversations or facts left without an owner.

Sizes are AWFlow's own measures. *Can free* is an estimate.

### Slim, archive and delete

Every action shows what it will free **before** it runs.

| Action | What it does | What you keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Slim** | Removes heavy data the agent no longer needs: tool result payloads (a one-line summary is kept), build snapshots except the last 3, and regenerated answers you didn't pick. You can **Export a full copy first**. | Every message, which tools ran, the summary. The conversation stays readable. |
| **Archive** | Saves the conversations to an `.awmem` file (optionally with a passphrase), then removes them from this browser. | A file you can [import](#export-and-import) again. |
| **Delete** | Removes the conversations. Workflows that use them start with an empty memory. Can't be undone. | Nothing. |
| **Clear orphans** | Removes conversations and facts whose agent, team or workflow was deleted. | Shared memory keys and anything a workflow still uses. |

When memory gets large, AWFlow suggests what to free. See [Storage & clean-up](/usage/using-the-app/memory/storage/).

## Export and import

Memory is exported as an **`.awmem`** file:

- **Export all** (top of the page) saves every conversation and fact on this device.
- **Export** on a conversation, a selection or an owner's facts saves just those.
- Tick **Protect with a passphrase** to encrypt the file. Use at least the minimum length shown; you need the passphrase to import it, and it can't be recovered if you forget it.

**Import** opens an `.awmem` file. If it is protected, enter its passphrase. A preview shows how many conversations, messages and facts it holds and when it was exported; untick what you don't want. Import **never overwrites anything**: every conversation is added as a copy, and facts you already have are skipped.

Use export and import to move your memory to another browser or device, or to keep a copy before clearing.

## When you delete an agent, a team or a workflow

- **Agent or team** — the delete dialog asks **Also delete its N memories** (on by default) and shows one of them. Uncheck it to keep them: they then appear under **Facts → No owner**. An agent's chats stay in **Memory → Conversations**.
- **Workflow** — if the workflow has its own chat memory, the dialog offers **Delete its conversations** (the default) or **Keep it in Memory**, with the number of messages and the size. Conversations under a **shared** key, and conversations another workflow also uses, are always kept, and the dialog says so.
- **Workflow with canvas dock chats** — its saved dock chats are part of its own conversations and are offered for deletion with it.

## Encryption on this device

{/* TODO(#1262) verify: wording and behaviour once vault encryption at rest has shipped. */}

When **Local secret protection** is on (**Settings › Preferences**, the same passphrase that protects your credentials), conversation messages and facts are **encrypted on this device** too.

- Titles, sizes, dates and kinds stay readable, so the lists still work.
- To read messages or facts, **unlock** with your passphrase. While locked, the page shows **Unlock to read memory**.
- Searching inside messages, and picking the most relevant facts for a chat, work only while unlocked. While locked, chats fall back to your newest facts.
- Turning protection off decrypts everything in place.

## Plans

Conversations are **not limited by your plan**. Plans only limit what is stored on our servers, and memory is stored on your device. The only limit is the space your browser allows: see [Storage & clean-up](/usage/using-the-app/memory/storage/).
