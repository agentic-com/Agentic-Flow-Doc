The **request board** is where you ask for a workflow you can't find — and watch it get
built. Describe what you want, other people vote it up, a maintainer picks it up, and when
it ships you get a notification that links straight to the ready-to-use workflow.

## Finding the board

Two ways in:

- **Sidebar → Request Board** (under *Published Workflows*).
- The **"What the community wants built"** banner near the top of the
  [Marketplace](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/publishing/), which teases the most-wanted
  requests and links through.

You can also reach it when a Marketplace search finds nothing — a *"see what others have
requested →"* link appears with the empty result.

:::note
The request board is a shared, community space, so it needs an account. In anonymous
(local-only) mode you can't post or vote — your local workflows stay on your device.
:::

## Requesting a workflow

1. Click **Request a workflow** (on the board, or from the gallery's *"Can't find it?"*
   tile).
2. Give it a **Title** (e.g. *"Auto-summarize my inbox every morning"*) and a
   **Description** of what it should do and why.
3. Optionally pick a **Category** to help others find it.
4. **Submit request.**

### "Someone already asked for this"

As you type a title, the board looks for **similar requests** and shows them:

> **2 similar requests** — join instead of duplicating?

If one matches what you want, click **Join +1** to add your vote instead of creating a
duplicate — you'll still be notified when it ships. This keeps demand visible in one place
rather than scattered across near-identical entries. If none fit, just finish your new
request.

## Reading the board

Requests move through three lanes:

| Lane | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 **Most wanted** | Open requests, sorted by votes. Click **Join +1** to add yours. |
| **Building now** | Someone's on it. When it's close, an **In review** badge and an *"Est. ship in …"* countdown appear. |
| **Shipped** | Done. Each card links to **Open the workflow →** and shows when it shipped. |

A **heartbeat bar** at the top shows the board's momentum — requests and votes this week,
what shipped this month, and how many are in review.

## Getting notified when it ships

When a request you made *or voted on* ships, you get:

- an **in-app notification** — the bell in the top bar shows an unread count; open it to
  see *"Your requested workflow shipped"* and jump straight to the workflow;
- an **email** (if you're signed in with email notifications), with a link that opens the
  ready workflow in a couple of clicks.

Every requester and every voter on a request is notified — so joining a request with
**Join +1** is also how you subscribe to its "it shipped" ping.

## For maintainers

Workspace moderators manage requests (status, estimated ship date, marking a request
fulfilled and linking the published workflow) and merge duplicate requests from the admin
request screen. See
[Featuring and curating listings](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/publishing/#admin-featuring-a-listing)
for the related Marketplace curation controls.
