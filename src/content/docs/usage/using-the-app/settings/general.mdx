**Settings › General** holds your account profile. You need to be signed in to use it. In a local workspace the page only offers a **Sign in** button; see [Local or cloud](/usage/using-the-app/account/local-vs-cloud/).

{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings › General at #/app/settings/general while signed in, showing avatar, username, tagline, password and newsletter cards */}

## Avatar

Click your avatar and pick **Upload a photo...**, then crop it. **Remove photo** goes back to the default.

## Username

Type a new name in **Username** and click **Update**.

## Publisher tagline

A short line (up to 160 characters) shown next to your name on the Marketplace when you publish workflows. Click **Save**.

## Password

Fill in **Old Password**, **New Password** and **Confirm Password**, then click **Update**. Use the eye icon to show what you typed.

If you forgot your current password, sign out and use **Forgot your password?** on the login screen. See [Reset your password](/usage/using-the-app/account/reset-password/).

## Newsletter

Turn **Subscribe to the newsletter** on or off. You get occasional product updates and community highlights. Security and password emails are always sent, whatever you choose here. See [Newsletter](/usage/using-the-app/newsletter/).

## Delete your account

There is no delete button in the app. To have your account and cloud data erased, email **hello@awflow.io**. See [Privacy & data](/usage/privacy-and-data/#delete-your-data).
