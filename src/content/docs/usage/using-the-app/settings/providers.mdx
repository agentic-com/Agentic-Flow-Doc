**Settings › Providers** is where you connect cloud AI services for Aria and your agents. Without a provider, the assistant can still use [on-device models](/usage/using-the-app/local-models/).

## Add a provider

<Steps>

1. Click **+ Add provider**.

2. Pick the **Provider**: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, xAI, Azure OpenAI, Ollama (local) or OpenAI-compatible.

3. Give it a **Label** you'll recognise, paste your **API key**, and type the **Model** name.

4. For Ollama or an OpenAI-compatible service, fill in the **Base URL**. For Azure, fill in the instance and deployment names.

5. Optionally set **Order** (lower numbers are tried first), **Set as default**, and a **Max output tokens** cap.

6. Click **Add**.

</Steps>

Each provider has an on/off switch, **Edit** and **Delete**.

<Aside>
Providers and their keys are stored **on this device only**, encrypted, and never synced to your account. The assistant calls the provider straight from your browser with your key. Add them again on each device you use.
</Aside>
