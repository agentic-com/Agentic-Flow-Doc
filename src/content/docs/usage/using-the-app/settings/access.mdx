**Settings › Site access** lists the websites (hosts) and connected apps each workflow is allowed to use.

## How access is granted

You don't add entries here. A workflow asks the first time it needs a website or an app's credential, and you choose to allow it. If you pick **Always allow for this workflow**, the permission is saved and shows up on this page.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Settings › Site access at #/app/settings/access with two workflow cards listing hosts and apps */}

## Review and change access

Each workflow has a card with its hosts and, under **APPS**, its apps.

- **Switch off** a host or app to stop the access. The workflow asks again the next time it needs it. Switch it back on to restore it.
- The **trash** icon removes the permission for good.
- **Remove all** clears every permission for that workflow.

Use the search box to find a host, an app or a workflow, and the **All**, **Enabled** and **Disabled** tabs to filter.

A workflow you deleted appears as *Removed workflow (no longer exists)*. You can remove its entries.

<Aside type="caution">
These permissions always apply to workflows you installed. For workflows **you created**, they only apply when [Safe mode](/usage/using-the-app/settings/security/) is on. A banner reminds you when it's off.
</Aside>

Permissions are saved **on this device only**.

To clear all permissions of an installed workflow at once, you can also use **Reset permissions** in its row menu on the [workflows list](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/manage-list/).
