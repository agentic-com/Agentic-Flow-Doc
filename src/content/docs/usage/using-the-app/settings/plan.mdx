**Settings › Plan** shows the plan you're on and compares all plans side by side.

<Aside>
Paid plans aren't on sale yet: their buttons read **Coming soon**. The Plan section is not listed in Settings for every account yet.
</Aside>

## What plans limit

Plans only limit what you store **in the cloud**. A [local workspace](/usage/using-the-app/account/local-vs-cloud/) is free and has no limits on workflows, credentials or memories, apart from the space your browser allows.

The comparison lists, for each plan:

- **Usage limits**: number of workflows, saved versions per workflow, steps per workflow, runs kept, and saved credentials.
- **Storage & integrations**: cloud sync, export and import, HTTP requests and pre-built integrations.
- **Page interaction & automation nodes**, **Triggers & workflow automation**, and **Support & service**.

Prices are shown in euros, per month or per year. On a narrow screen, switch plans with the tabs and click **Show all features**; on a wide screen, click **Show detailed comparison**.

## What happens at a limit

- When you reach the workflow or credential limit, you can't create more in the cloud. You can still create them locally, or move some to local storage. See [Manage your workflows list](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/manage-list/#local-or-cloud-storage).
- When you reach the limit on kept runs, the oldest cloud runs are removed to make room. See [Run history](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/run-history/#how-long-runs-are-kept).

## Switch plan

The free plan shows **Switch to …** and applies at once. Your current plan shows **Current plan**.

In a local workspace this page asks you to sign in first.
