Clicking the Agentic WorkFlow icon in your browser toolbar opens the app in Chrome's **side panel**, next to the page you're working on. You can keep the page and your workflows side by side: pick elements, run a workflow, and watch its results without switching tabs.

The side panel starts narrow (about 320–450px), and you can drag its left edge to make it wider. The app adapts to whatever width you give it.

## Opening it

- Click the **Agentic WorkFlow** icon in the toolbar. If you don't see it, open the puzzle-piece **Extensions** menu and pin it.
- Click the icon again, or use the panel's close button, to hide it. Your work stays where you left it.

## What changes when the panel is narrow

To fit the panel, the app rearranges itself instead of cutting things off:

- **Header.** It shows the name of the page you're on, a **search** icon (it opens the same search as <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd>), and notifications. The sidebar button (top left) opens the full navigation.
- **Lists** (workflows, credentials, knowledges, chat memories, published workflows). Each item is a compact two-line row with its status and a **⋯** menu. The **New** button is always visible next to the tabs.
- **Forms** (new credential, new knowledge…). They open as a full-height sheet, with the Save/Create button pinned to the bottom.
- **Workflow canvas.**
  - The **Editor / Logs / Runs** switch gets its own row under the header.
  - The canvas tools sit in a single vertical rail.
  - **Add a node** opens as a sheet from the bottom.
  - Node settings open as a full-height sheet.
  - The minimap is hidden until the panel is wide enough.
- **Runs.** Tap a run in the list to open its details, and use **‹ Runs** to go back.
- **Settings.** **Settings** shows a list of sections with their current state (for example "Usage stats on"). Tap one to open it, and use **‹ Settings** to go back.
- **Assistant.** Your chat history opens as a drawer from the history icon.
- **Marketplace.** On a workflow's page, the **Install** button stays pinned at the bottom of the panel while you read about it.

## Making it wider

Drag the panel's left edge to widen it. At around **450px**, headers fit on one line. From around **670px**, full tables, the canvas minimap, split views and side-by-side layouts come back, as in a regular tab.
