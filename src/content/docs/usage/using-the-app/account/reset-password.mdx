import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Steps>

1. On the login screen, click **Forgot your password?**

2. Enter the **email** address of your account and click **Submit**.

3. Open the email we sent and click the reset link.

4. Enter a **New Password** and **Confirm Password**, then click **Reset Password**.

5. When you see *Password has been reset*, click **Login to your account**.

</Steps>

If the reset page says *Unexpected Error*, the link is incomplete or out of date. Ask for a new one.

To change a password you still know, use [Settings › General](/usage/using-the-app/settings/general/#password) instead.
