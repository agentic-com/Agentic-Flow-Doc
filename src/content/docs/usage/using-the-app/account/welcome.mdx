When you install the extension, AWFlow opens a **welcome page** once. You don't need an account; in a local workspace it shows *Local · no account needed*.

{/* SCREENSHOT: Welcome page at #/app/welcome in a local workspace, before running "Start here" */}

## Start here

Click **Run "Start here"**. A three-step workflow picks a quote and hands it back in about five seconds. Nothing leaves your device.

## Or pick your own way in

- **Start from a template**: choose one of the ready-made workflows.
- **Describe it to the AI**: tell Aria what you want, for example "Every morning, send me…". See [Building workflows in chat](/usage/using-the-app/chat-and-agents/building-workflows-in-chat/).
- **2-minute tutorial**: build one yourself, step by step.

**Skip to the app →** takes you to the dashboard.
