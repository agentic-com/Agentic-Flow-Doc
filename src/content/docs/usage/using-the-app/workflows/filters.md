The **Workflows** page lets you narrow the list to just the workflows you care about. Filters sit in the toolbar above the table, next to the search box. Your selections — along with sorting, visible columns and rows-per-page — are **remembered per table** and re-applied automatically the next time you open the page.

## Available filters

Each filter is a multi-select: pick one or more values, and the list shows workflows matching any of them.

- **Status** — **Active** or **Inactive**.
- **Storage** — where the workflow is saved: **Cloud** (your account) or **Local** (this device only).
- **Published** — whether the workflow is published to the marketplace: **Published** or **Not published**.
- **Tags** — narrow by tag. See [Workflow tags](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/tags/). The Tags filter only appears once at least one workflow carries a tag.

You can combine filters (for example, **Active** + **Local**) to narrow further, and use the search box on top for free-text matching.

## Filter the list

1. On the **Workflows** page, open a filter (for example **Storage**) above the table.
2. Select one or more values. A count badge on the filter shows how many are active, and the table updates immediately.
3. Use **Clear filters** inside a filter to reset just that one.

## Filters are remembered

Your filters, sort order, chosen columns and page size are saved in the browser for each list. When you reload the app or come back later, the list opens exactly as you left it.

> **Note:** This is stored on the device you are using, so it does not follow you to another browser or device. Clearing your browser data resets it to the defaults.
