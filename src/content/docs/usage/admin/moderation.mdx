**Moderation** (Admin panel › Marketplace › **Moderation**) is the review queue for workflows people publish to the Marketplace. Nothing goes public until a moderator approves it.

## The queue

- **Pending**: new listings and updates to live listings, oldest first. Updates carry an **Update** badge.
- **Approved**: live listings, most recently updated first. Click **Load more live listings** to see more.

Search by name, description, publisher or tags, and filter by **Tags**, **Publisher**, **Language**, **Featured**, and the **Submitted** and **Updated** dates.

## Review a listing

<Steps>

1. Click **Review** on a row.

2. Look at the read-only graph. It is the exact version people will install. For an update, switch between **Proposed** and **Live** to compare.

3. Check the permissions panel. A red warning means the permissions the author declared don't match what the workflow really does.

4. Read the listing content: icon, screenshots, description, tags and categories.

5. Optionally click **Open sandbox to test** to run a throwaway copy safely. It uses no saved accounts, opens a fresh tab, asks before every site, and shows all network activity. Use test accounts, not real ones.

6. Tick the **Review checklist**. The first three checks are required before you can approve.

7. Click **Approve snapshot…** (or **Approve update**), **Reject…** or **Suspend…**. Rejecting and suspending need a note the author will see.

</Steps>

If the author changes the listing while you review it, your decision isn't saved and you are asked to load the new version.

From the queue you can also reject or suspend several listings at once. Bulk approve only works for AWFlow's own official imports.

## Suspend a live listing

**Suspend…** needs a reason and a note. The reason decides what happens to installed copies, the same way as for [Reports](/usage/admin/reports/): *Malware or data theft* and *Safety risk* stop every installed copy; *Policy* hides the listing but lets installed copies keep running with a notice.

## Feature a listing

On an approved listing with no pending update, click **Feature** to put it in the Marketplace's Featured banner. An optional **Rank** orders featured listings: lower numbers show first. **Unfeature** removes it.

More about featuring and what authors see: [Publishing › Admin: featuring a listing](/usage/using-the-app/workflows/publishing/#admin-featuring-a-listing).

<Aside>
Every decision, including the checklist you ticked, is recorded in the [Audit log](/usage/admin/audit-log/).
</Aside>
