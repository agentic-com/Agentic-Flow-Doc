**Reports** (Admin panel › Marketplace › **Reports**) lists every Marketplace listing with open reports from users.

Each row shows the **Listing**, how many **Reports** it has, and the **Reasons** given: *Spam*, *Malware / security*, *Inappropriate*, *Copyright* or *Other*. Click **view N reports** to read each report's details and who sent it.

## Handle a report

<Steps>

1. On the listing's row, open **⋮ › Moderate…**.

2. To keep the listing, click **Dismiss reports**. A note is optional.

3. To take it down, pick a **Reason**, write a **Note**, and optionally a **Message to people who installed it**. Then click **Take down**.

</Steps>

The reason decides what happens to people who already installed the workflow:

| Reason | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Malware or data theft** | Every installed copy stops and its triggers turn off. |
| **Safety risk** | Every installed copy stops and its triggers turn off. |
| **Policy** | The listing is hidden. Installed copies keep running, with a notice. |

<Aside>
People who installed the workflow see your message in their notice. Keep it short and plain.
</Aside>

The decision is recorded in the [Audit log](/usage/admin/audit-log/).
