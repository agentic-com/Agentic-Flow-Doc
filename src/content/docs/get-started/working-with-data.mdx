Level 2 is about the data that flows between steps: reading it, reshaping it, deciding what to do with it, and keeping it.

<Aside type="note" title="Lessons coming next">
The level 2 lessons, with steps, checks and progress tracking like [level 1](/get-started/install/), are not written yet. Until they are, each topic below links to the guide that covers it today, plus one longer walkthrough you can follow end to end.
</Aside>

## Planned lessons

| # | Lesson | What you'll learn | Learn it today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Use data from earlier steps | Pick fields by drag and drop, then write `{{ }}` expressions. | [Mapping in the UI](/concepts/data/data-mapping/data-mapping-ui/), [Expressions](/concepts/data/data-mapping/data-mapping-expressions/) |
| 2 | Work with lists of items | Why a step runs once per item, and how items stay linked. | [Data structure](/concepts/data/data-structure/), [Item linking](/concepts/data/item-linking/) |
| 3 | Take a different path | **If** and **Switch** on what a page contains. | [Walkthrough: branch on page content](/get-started/branch-on-page-content/), [Splitting](/concepts/flow/splitting/) |
| 4 | Repeat and wait | Loop over pages, wait for an element, merge branches. | [Looping](/concepts/flow/looping/), [Waiting](/concepts/flow/waiting/), [Merging](/concepts/flow/merging/) |
| 5 | Keep what you found | Save values between runs and export results. | [Data store](/app/data-store/), [Export & import](/app/workflows/export-import/) |
| 6 | When something fails | Read a failed run and make a workflow recover. | [Run history](/app/workflows/run-history/), [Error handling](/concepts/flow/error-handling/) |
