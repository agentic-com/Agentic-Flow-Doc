A **workflow** is a chain of steps, called **nodes**. A **trigger** starts it, and each node passes its output to the next one. You'll build the smallest useful workflow: read the text you select on a page, and turn it into a small report.

<FlowPreview src="/examples/first-workflow.awf" caption="What you'll build: When Started → Get Selected Text → Edit Fields." />

## Steps

<StepCard n={1} title="Create a blank workflow" check="an empty canvas opens with an Add Trigger button.">
  In the AWFlow side panel, open **Workflows** and click **Add Workflow**. In **Create a workflow**, choose **Blank canvas**, then **Triggered**. Name it `My first workflow` and click **Create & open**.
</StepCard>

<Screenshot
  src={createWorkflowLight}
  srcDark={createWorkflowDark}
  alt="The Create a workflow dialog, step 1 of 3 (Source, Choose, Details): Start from a template (recommended) at the top, then Blank canvas, which is selected, From the marketplace and Import, with Cancel and Continue buttons."

/>

<StepCard n={2} title="Add a trigger" check="a When Started node is on the canvas.">
  Click **Add Trigger** (in a narrow panel: **Add trigger** in the middle of the canvas). In **Choose a node**, type `when` in the search box and pick **When Started**. It starts the workflow when you press **Test Workflow**.
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={3} title="Read the selected text" check="Get Selected Text is linked to When Started by a line.">
  Click the **+** on the right edge of **When Started**, search for **Get Selected Text** and pick it. In its settings, set **Output field name** to `text`, then click **Done**.
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={4} title="Run it" check="Get Selected Text → Output → Actual shows a text field with your sentence.">
  Switch to your other tab and select (highlight) a sentence. Come back to the AWFlow panel and click **Test Workflow** at the top of the canvas. Then open **Get Selected Text** and look at its **Output** tab, under **Actual**.
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={5} title="Reshape it with Edit Fields" check="Edit Fields → Output → Actual shows processed_text with your sentence and its length.">
  Click the **+** on the right of **Get Selected Text** and add **Edit Fields**. Under **Fields**, add a field named `processed_text` of type *String*, and paste this as its value:

  ```text title="Value"
  Text: {{ $input.text }}
  Length: {{ $input.text.length }} characters
  ```

  `{{ $input.text }}` reads the `text` field from the step before. Click **Done**, select some text again, and click **Test Workflow**.
</StepCard>

<StepCard n={6} title="Save it" check="the header says Saved.">
  AWFlow doesn't save automatically: click **Save** in the canvas header, or press <kbd>⌘</kbd>/<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>S</kbd>. You'll reuse this canvas in the next lesson.
</StepCard>

<Aside type="tip" title="Test one step at a time">
Hover a node and click **Test this step** to run just that step, or open it and click **Test step**. Running often, one node at a time, is the fastest way to find what's wrong.
</Aside>

## What you've learned

- Every workflow has a **trigger** and one or more **nodes**, joined by lines that carry data.
- Each node's **Output** tab shows what it produced on the last run.
- `{{ $input.field }}` reads a field from the previous step. See [Expressions](/concepts/data/data-mapping/data-mapping-expressions/) for more.

The nodes in this lesson: [Get Selected Text](/nodes/extension/getselectedtext/) and [Edit Fields](/nodes/builtin/datatransformation/editfields/).
