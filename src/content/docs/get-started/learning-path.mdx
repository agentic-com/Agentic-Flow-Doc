The learning path takes you from installing AWFlow to building agents, one short lesson at a time. Every lesson ends with something that works, and tells you how to check it. Your progress is saved **in this browser**, with no account: open any lesson to see the progress ring in the left rail.

<YouTube id="ogZ03_AC5o8" />

**Available now.** About 30 minutes. You finish with an AI workflow that runs from a keyboard shortcut, and you know how to ask Aria for the next one.

## About your progress

- Progress is stored in your browser's local storage, on this device only. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- In a private window, or if your browser blocks site storage, the lessons still work, but progress is forgotten when you leave the page.
- To start over, clear this site's data in your browser settings.
