:::note[In 20 seconds]
**Wait** pauses for a set time and then carries on with the same data. **Wait For Approval** pauses until a person approves or rejects. **Poll Until** keeps checking a URL until it's ready. All three pause **once for everything they receive**, not once per item.
:::

## How long waits survive

Wait holds the run for **Amount** × **Unit** (seconds unless you change it):

- **Under 1 minute:** a simple timer. Closing the tab cancels it.
- **1 minute or more, in the browser extension:** the run is saved and resumes at the deadline, even after you close the tab or restart the browser. It shows as **waiting** in the Executions pane and can last up to **30 days**.
- **In the web app:** every wait is a simple timer, so a long wait doesn't survive a closed tab.

Wait For Approval has no time limit. The question appears on the paused run in the **Executions** pane, and you're told a run is waiting by a count in the top bar, a sound and, in the extension, a desktop notification.

## Wait for a page, not a clock

To wait until something appears on a web page, don't guess a delay. Use [Wait For Element](/nodes/extension/ui/wait-for-element/), so the run continues as soon as the page is ready. See [Browser context](/concepts/flow/browser-context/).

## You'll notice this when…

<details>
<summary>Ten items left a Wait together instead of one by one</summary>

Wait pauses once for everything it receives. To space items out, put Wait inside a [Loop](/nodes/builtin/flow/loop/) or after [Split in Batches](/nodes/builtin/flow/splitinbatches/).

</details>

<details>
<summary>A long wait was lost when you closed the tab</summary>

The run was in the web app, or the wait was shorter than a minute. Long waits survive a closed tab only in the browser extension.

</details>

<details>
<summary>One approval routed every item</summary>

Wait For Approval pauses once and one answer routes all the items it received. For one decision per item, place it inside a Loop or after Split in Batches with a batch size of 1.

</details>
